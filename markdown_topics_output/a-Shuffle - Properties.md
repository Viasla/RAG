# Properties
There are exactly a^n distinct a‑shuffles of an n‑card deck, establishing the cardinality of the a‑shuffle space.

The probability that an a‑shuffle yields a permutation with exactly r rising sequences (1 \le r \le a) is \[ \frac{\binom{n+a-r}{n}}{a^{n}}\,A(n,r) \], where A(n,r) is the Eulerian number.

The marginal probability that the first card moves to position i is given by the sum \[ \frac{(a-1)^{i-1}a^{n-i}+ (a-2)^{i-1}(a-1)^{n-i}+\dots+1^{i-1}2^{n-i}}{a^{n}} \].

When a=2 the a‑shuffle reduces to the familiar riffle (or Gilbert–Shannon–Reeds) shuffle, and the general formulas specialize to the known results for riffle shuffles.

The coding of a‑shuffles via n‑tuples provides an explicit bijection that can be used for algorithmic generation of random a‑shuffles and for exact enumeration of events such as the position of a given card.
