# Proof
The proof starts by writing E(X·Y) as a double sum over the sample spaces of X and Y: E(X·Y)=∑_j∑_k x_j y_k P(X=x_j, Y=y_k). Independence gives P(X=x_j, Y=y_k)=P(X=x_j)P(Y=y_k). Substituting and rearranging the double sum yields (∑_j x_j P(X=x_j))(∑_k y_k P(Y=y_k)) which are precisely E(X) and E(Y), so E(X·Y)=E(X)E(Y).
