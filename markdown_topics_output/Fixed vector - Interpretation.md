# Interpretation
The fixed row vector w is the stationary distribution of a regular Markov chain: after a long time the probability of being in state si approaches wi, independent of the initial state. The fixed column vector x is a constant vector (typically the all‑ones vector) reflecting that each column of P sums to one.

The components of the fixed vector represent the long‑run proportion of time the chain spends in each state; in the vowel‑consonant example they give the expected percentage of vowels and consonants in a very long text, and in general they give the equilibrium probabilities toward which the chain converges.
