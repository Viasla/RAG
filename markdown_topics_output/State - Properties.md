# Properties
For each state $s_i$, the sum of the outgoing transition probabilities equals one: $\sum_{j}p_{ij}=1$.

The probability of remaining in the same state is $p_{ii}$, which may be positive.

The collection of states together with $P$ completely determines the probabilistic behavior of the chain; any future distribution can be computed by multiplying the initial distribution by powers of $P$.

If the chain has a finite set of states and the transition matrix is regular (some power of $P$ has all positive entries), long‑run probabilities become independent of the initial state.
