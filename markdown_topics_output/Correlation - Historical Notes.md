# Historical Notes
Exercise 18 introduces the correlation coefficient ρ(X,Y) as the covariance of X and Y divided by the product of their standard deviations, formalizing a measure of linear dependence between random variables.

In Exercise 20 the bivariate normal density is expressed with a parameter ρ, and part (b) shows that this parameter is precisely the correlation of X and Y, illustrating the historical use of ρ in joint normal distributions.
