# Interpretation
The equality $E(T^B)=BB$ demonstrates that the expected duration of the game (number of tosses until the target pattern appears) and the expected financial outlay to gamblers are directly linked through the martingale property.  This relation allows one to compute expected waiting times for patterns by determining the payout structure of the associated gambling scheme, or conversely, to verify the fairness of a betting scheme by checking that the expected payout equals the expected number of trials.
