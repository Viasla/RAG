# Historical Notes
The concept of a conditional density function arises in the context of continuous sample spaces, extending the discrete notion of conditional probability. It is defined using the original density f(x) and the probability of the conditioning event E, mirroring the discrete formula P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B). The text presents a formal definition in the section titled “Continuous Conditional Probability” and applies it to several familiar problems such as the spinner and dart game.

The definition and use of conditional densities in continuous settings are illustrated in Example 4.20, which connects the memoryless property of the exponential distribution to a practical radioactive decay experiment. This shows a historical link between the mathematical formalism and real-world stochastic processes.
