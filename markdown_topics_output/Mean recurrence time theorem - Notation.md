# Notation
$s_i$ – a state of the Markov chain

$r_i$ – mean recurrence time for state $s_i$

$w_i$ – $i$‑th component of the stationary (fixed) probability vector $\mathbf w$

$\mathbf D$ – diagonal matrix with $d_{ii}=r_i$ (mean recurrence matrix)

$\mathbf M$ – mean first‑passage matrix with entries $m_{ij}$ (off‑diagonal) and $m_{ii}=0$

$\mathbf P$ – transition matrix of the chain

$\mathbf I$ – identity matrix

$\mathbf C$ – matrix of all ones

$\mathbf w$ – row vector of stationary probabilities
