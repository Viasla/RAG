# Proof
Property 1 follows because m(ω)≥0 for all ω and P(E)=∑_{ω∈E}m(ω).

Property 2 is proved by summing m(ω) over all ω∈Ω, yielding 1.

Property 3 is proved by noting that the sum over E is a subset of the sum over F, so ∑_{ω∈E}m(ω)≤∑_{ω∈F}m(ω).

Property 4 is proved by expanding P(A∪B)=∑_{ω∈A∪B}m(ω)=∑_{ω∈A}m(ω)+∑_{ω∈B}m(ω) since A and B are disjoint.

Property 5 is proved using the disjoint union Ω=A∪Â and the additivity of Property 4 to obtain 1=P(A)+P(Â), hence P(Â)=1−P(A).
