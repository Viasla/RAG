# Interpretation
The density function f(x) = e^{-x} is plotted to compare with the histogram of values obtained by taking the negative logarithm of a uniformly distributed random number in [0,1].

Density functions are interpreted as the continuous analogue of probability mass functions, assigning probability per unit length to outcomes, as reflected by the various specific density entries.

The joint density function interpretation involves the product of marginal densities when variables are independent, suggested by its separate listing.
