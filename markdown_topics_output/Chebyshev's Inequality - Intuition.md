# Intuition
Chebyshev’s Inequality expresses the idea that a random variable cannot stray far from its mean with high probability unless its variance is large; the bound becomes tighter as the variance decreases or as the tolerated deviation ε increases.

In the context of Bernoulli trials, the inequality tells us that the average of many independent trials will, with high probability, stay close to the true success probability, because the variance of the average shrinks as 1/n.

Chebyshev's inequality is highlighted as a tool to estimate probabilities for a large number of independent Bernoulli trials when the Central Limit Theorem is also available for comparison.
