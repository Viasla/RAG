# Formal Definition
The statistic \(\chi^2\) is defined as \(\displaystyle \chi^2\;=\;\sum_{i=1}^{8}\frac{(O_i-E_i)^2}{E_i}\), where \(O_i\) are the observed counts in the eight cells of a contingency table and \(E_i\) are the corresponding expected counts under the hypothesis of independence. Under the independence assumption, the probability density of this random variable is approximately the chi‑squared density, a special case of the gamma density.
