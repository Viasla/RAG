# Examples
1. For the set $A={a,b,c}$ a permutation can be written in two‑row notation as $sigma=egin{pmatrix} a & b & c \ b & c & a end{pmatrix}$, which sends $a$ to $b$, $b$ to $c$, and $c$ to $a$.

2. All possible permutations of the numbers ${1,2,3}$ are the six rearrangements: 123, 132, 213, 231, 312, 321.

3. For the set $A={a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4}$ the permutation $sigma=egin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 \ 2 & 1 & 4 & 3 end{pmatrix}$ sends $a_1$ to $a_2$, $a_2$ to $a_1$, $a_3$ to $a_4$, and $a_4$ to $a_3$.

4. A 2‑permutation (ordered list of two distinct elements) from the set ${a,b,c,d}$ could be $a,b$ or $c,d$, etc.
