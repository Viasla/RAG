# Interpretation
Prop. III formalizes the idea that expectations for games with unequal outcome probabilities can be determined by treating the probabilities as ratios of equal‑chance elementary events. It shows that the expectation is the weighted average of the outcomes, with weights reflecting the relative frequencies of the events. This reasoning underlies the modern definition of expected value as a sum of outcomes each multiplied by its probability, and illustrates Huygens' early use of combinatorial symmetry to infer general results from particular symmetric cases.
