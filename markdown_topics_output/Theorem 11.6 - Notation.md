# Notation
(s_i) – transient state, (s_j) – absorbing state; (p_{ij}) – one‑step transition probability from (s_i) to (s_j); (b_{ij}) – probability of eventual absorption in (s_j) starting from (s_i); (mathbf{Q}) – transition matrix restricted to transient states; (mathbf{R}) – transition matrix from transient to absorbing states; (mathbf{I}) – identity matrix; (mathbf{N}=(mathbf{I}-mathbf{Q})^{-1}) – fundamental matrix.
