# Proof
1. By symmetry, \(P(S_1>0,S_2>0,\dots,S_{2m}>0)=\tfrac12\,P(S_1\neq0,S_2\neq0,\dots,S_{2m}\neq0)\). The right‑hand side is the probability that the walk never returns to the origin in the first \(2m\) steps.

2. Exercise 7 in the text shows that the probability of no equalisation (no return to the origin) in a walk of length \(2m\) is precisely \(u_{2m}\).

3. If a walk has no equalisation, its first step must be +1 (otherwise it would immediately be negative). Conversely, any walk that stays non‑negative for all \(2m\) steps necessarily has no equalisation and starts with +1. Hence the events "no equalisation" and "stay non‑negative" are in one‑to‑one correspondence.

4. Combining 1 and 2 gives \(P(S_1\ge0,\dots,S_{2m}\ge0)=u_{2m}\).
