# Examples
For the maze example (Example 11.22) the stationary vector is w = (1/12, 1/8, 1/12, 1/8, 1/6, 1/8, 1/12, 1/8, 1/12). The corresponding mean recurrence times are r = (12, 8, 12, 8, 6, 8, 12, 8, 12), i.e., the average number of steps needed to return to each compartment.

In the Land of Oz weather model with states rain, nice, and snow, the limiting vector is w = (2/5, 1/5, 2/5). The mean recurrence times are therefore 1/(2/5)=5/2 days between rainy days, 1/(1/5)=5 days between nice days, and 5/2 days between snowy days.

In exercise 5 the mean time between occurrences of a given number in a fair six‑sided die is shown to be 6 steps, illustrating a basic mean recurrence calculation in a simple Markov chain.  In exercise 4 the mean recurrence times for each state of Example 11.4 (with a=0.5, b=0.75 and in general) are requested, highlighting how the concept applies to a more complex two‑parameter chain.  The “Land of Oz” example in exercise 7 is used to compute expected steps before reaching a particular room, which is a special case of mean recurrence if the starting and target rooms coincide.
