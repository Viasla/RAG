# Proof
The proof for bounded variables: assume X∈[a,b], Y∈[c,d] with densities f_X,f_Y. Independence gives joint density f_{X,Y}(x,y)=f_X(x)f_Y(y). Then E(XY)=∫_a^b∫_c^d xy f_X(x)f_Y(y)dy dx = (∫_a^b x f_X(x)dx)(∫_c^d y f_Y(y)dy)=E(X)E(Y). The general case uses approximating sequences of bounded variables; details are omitted.
