# Examples
Example 7.3 considers X and Y chosen independently and uniformly on [0,1].  Their densities are f_X(x)=f_Y(x)=1 on [0,1] and 0 otherwise.  The convolution yields f_Z(z)=z for 0≤z≤1, f_Z(z)=2−z for 1<z≤2, and f_Z(z)=0 otherwise, producing the familiar triangular distribution for the sum of two independent uniforms.  Example 7.4 examines X and Y chosen independently with exponential density λe^{−λx} for x≥0.  While the full convolution calculation is omitted, it demonstrates the same principle applied to exponential distributions.
