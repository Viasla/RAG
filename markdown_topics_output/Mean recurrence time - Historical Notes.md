# Historical Notes
The mean recurrence time was introduced as a quantity closely related to the mean first passage time, with its formal relationship to the stationary distribution established in Theorem 11.15, which states that for an ergodic Markov chain the mean recurrence time for state s_i is r_i = 1 / w_i, where w_i is the i‑th component of the fixed (stationary) probability vector.

The mean recurrence time is addressed in the exercises of the text, specifically in exercise 5 where the author asks to show that for a die rolled repeatedly the mean time between occurrences of a given number is 6, and in exercise 4 which requires computing the mean recurrence times for each state in Example 11.4 for specific parameter values a=0.5 and b=0.75 as well as in the general case.

The discussion of mean recurrence time appears alongside fundamental matrices and mean first passage times, indicating its central role in finite Markov chain theory.
