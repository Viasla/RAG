# Examples
The event D that “it will snow tomorrow or it will rain the next day” is expressed as D = B ∪ C, illustrating how union captures inclusive disjunction of events.

The event E that either A or C wins is described as E = A ∪ C, leading to the probability calculation P(E) = P(A)+P(C).

In the coin‑toss example, the event ‘at least one head turns up’ would be represented by a union of the individual head‑occurring outcomes, though the text labels it as the complement of the single outcome TTT.
