# Interpretation
The right‑hand side is precisely the discrete convolution of the sequences \{f_{2k}\} and \{u_{2k}\}. Thus the theorem asserts that the sequence \{u_{2n}\} is the convolution of itself with the first‑return sequence \{f_{2n}\}. In generating‑function terms this yields the relation U(x)=1+U(x)F(x), where U and F are the ordinary generating functions of \{u_{2n}\} and \{f_{2n}\} respectively.
