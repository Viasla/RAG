# Intuition
The variance addition formula reveals that when two random variables are added, the variability of the resulting sum can be greater, equal to, or smaller than the sum of their individual variances, depending on how they co‑vary. If the variables move in the same direction (positive covariance), the sum is more variable; if they move oppositely (negative covariance), the sum can be less variable. Independence eliminates this extra term, restoring the simple additive property.
