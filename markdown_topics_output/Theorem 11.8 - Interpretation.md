# Interpretation
The theorem guarantees that a regular or ergodic Markov chain possesses exactly one stationary (fixed) row probability vector, which is strictly positive. This vector is the unique solution of wP=w and provides the long‑run distribution of the chain regardless of the initial state.

The theorem guarantees that the only eigenvectors associated with eigenvalue 1 for an ergodic chain are multiples of the all‑ones vector, implying a unique stationary distribution up to scaling.

Consequently, any invariant probability distribution must assign positive probability to every state.
