# Properties
No properties, such as expected gains, risk characteristics, or limitations of the Labouchere system, are described in the text.

1. If the process ever terminates (the list becomes empty), the total amount won equals the sum of the numbers in the original list, which for the example 1+2+3+4 equals 10. 2. Simulation may show that the process does not always terminate, because a long run of losses can continually enlarge the list, leading to unbounded bet sizes. 3. Consequently, the system is not fool‑proof: although it promises a fixed profit if it terminates, the risk of never terminating (or requiring bets larger than available capital) means it does not guarantee a win in practice.
