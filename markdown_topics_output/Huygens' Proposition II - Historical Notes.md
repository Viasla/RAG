# Historical Notes
Huygens' Proposition II appears in his 1692 work ‘Calculating in Games of Chance’. It is part of the first systematic study of expected value, extending Pascal’s approach from two outcomes (Prop. I) to three outcomes (Prop. II) and later to an arbitrary number n. The method relies on symmetry arguments and was used by Huygens to formalize expected value before a rigorous probabilistic framework existed.
