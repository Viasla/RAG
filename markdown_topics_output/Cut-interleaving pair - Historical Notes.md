# Historical Notes
The concept of a cut‑interleaving pair arises in the analysis of riffle shuffles, where a shuffle is modelled as first cutting a deck into a labelled stacks (the "cut") and then interleaving the stacks (the "interleaving"). The terminology is used throughout the classic treatment of a‑shuffles in the literature on card shuffling and appears explicitly in Theorem 3.10 and its proof.
