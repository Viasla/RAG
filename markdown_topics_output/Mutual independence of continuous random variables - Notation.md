# Notation
F(x1,…,xn)=P(X1≤x1,…,Xn≤xn) is the joint cumulative distribution function of the random vector (X1,…,Xn).

f(x1,…,xn)=∂^nF/∂x1…∂xn is the joint probability density function.

Fi(x)=P(Xi≤x) and fi(x) are the marginal CDF and density of Xi respectively.

Theorem 4.3 uses continuous functions φi(x) to state that φ1(X1),…,φn(Xn) remain mutually independent when the Xi are independent.
