# Intuition
The Law of Cosines generalizes the Pythagorean theorem to non-right triangles, relating side lengths and an included angle. For a chord in a unit circle, the two radii forming the triangle have unit length, and the angle between them is simply the central angle subtending the chord. Thus, as the angle increases from 0 to \(\pi\), the chord length increases from 0 to the diameter 2, following the function \(L=\sqrt{2-2\cos\alpha}\), which is consistent with the geometric relationship that a chord of a circle is determined by the central angle.
