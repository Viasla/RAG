# Historical Notes
Theorem 9.3 is discussed in the context of the Central Limit Theorem for bounded discrete random variables; a complete formal proof can be found in Gnedenko and Kolmogorov's *Limit Distributions for Sums of Independent Random Variables* (Reading: Addison‑Wesley, 1968), p. 233.

The text explicitly states that the authors chose to ignore the technical requirement that the greatest common divisor of the differences of all possible values of the random variable be 1, a point that is addressed in the full proof by Gnedenko and Kolmogorov.
