# Notation
r_i denotes the mean recurrence time for state s_i.

The mean recurrence matrix D is the diagonal matrix with entries d_{ii}=r_i and all off‑diagonal entries zero.

m_{ij} – mean first passage time from state i to state j.  When i=j, m_{ii} is the mean recurrence time to state i.  Z – fundamental matrix of the Markov chain.  z_{ij} – ij‑th entry of Z.  w_i – stationary probability of state i.  μ̄_i – mean time until the next occurrence of state s_i when the chain starts in equilibrium.
