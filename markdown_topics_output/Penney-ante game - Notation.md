# Notation
A, B – distinct binary patterns of H/T chosen by player 1 and player 2 respectively.
AA, BB – total payoff a gambler would receive if pattern A or B, respectively, occurs (sum of successive fair bets 2,4,8,…). 
AB – total payoff earned by a gambler who starts when pattern A is being formed and then bets until pattern B occurs.
BA – analogous payoff when starting with pattern B and betting toward A.
p_A, p_B – probabilities that A or B appears first.
E(T^B) – expected waiting time (in tosses) for the first occurrence of pattern B starting from the empty history.
E_A(T^B) – expected additional waiting time for B given that the current suffix matches A.
E(T^{A;or;B}) – expected time until either A or B appears for the first time.
"\" denotes concatenation of symbols; "\emptyset" denotes the empty initial state.
