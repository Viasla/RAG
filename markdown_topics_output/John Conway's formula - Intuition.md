# Intuition
The formula arises from comparing the expected waiting times for the two competing patterns. By treating each pattern as a “gamble” that pays an amount equal to the expected waiting time (BB for pattern B, AA for pattern A) and using the linearity of expectation together with the fact that the total expected time until one of the patterns appears can be written in two ways—once conditioned on the first player winning and once on the second player winning—the odds emerge as a ratio of differences of these expected payouts.
