# Interpretation
The a‑unshuffle provides a concrete way to analyse the randomness introduced by repeated shuffling: by counting how many distinct a‑unshuffles exist (a^n) and how they act on individual cards, one can compute variation distances and probabilities of specific events.

In probability calculations, the a‑unshuffle coding makes it possible to derive explicit formulas, such as the probability that the first card moves to the i‑th position after an a‑shuffle, which is given by a sum of terms involving powers of (a‑k).
