# Historical Notes
The point count method for evaluating a bridge hand is a simplified form of a more complex evaluation system that also takes into account voids in suits and other hand characteristics. This simplified method assigns numeric values to face cards: ace 4, king 3, queen 2, jack 1, and all other cards 0. The concept of point count was further discussed in R. A. Epstein’s book *The Theory of Gambling and Statistical Logic* (1977).
