# Intuition
Theorem 11.9 generalizes the convergence result for a regular Markov chain to the case where the initial state is not a single state but an arbitrary probability vector v. It states that, regardless of the starting distribution, the distribution after many steps converges to the unique stationary distribution w, provided the chain is regular. This reflects the idea that the long–run behavior of a regular Markov chain is independent of its starting distribution.
