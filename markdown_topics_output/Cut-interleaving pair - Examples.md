# Examples
For a=2 and n=4, the base‑2 integer 0101 corresponds to the cut‑interleaving pair in which the first and third cards come from stack 0 and the second and fourth cards come from stack 1. Applying this pair to the identity ordering (1,2,3,4) yields the permutation (1,3,2,4).

If a=3 and n=5, the base‑3 integer 20102 describes a cut‑interleaving pair where the sequence of stack labels is (2,0,1,0,2). The resulting ordering is obtained by cutting the deck into three stacks according to the counts of each label and then interleaving them according to that label sequence.
