# Proof
The proof proceeds by first establishing that E[S_j^{(n)}]=n w_j+o(n) for an ergodic chain. Next, one shows that the asymptotic variance of S_j^{(n)} equals σ_j^2 n+o(n) using the fundamental matrix of the chain, where z_{jj} denotes the jth diagonal entry of the deviation matrix. With these two asymptotic moments in hand, a martingale or mixing‑CLT argument for Markov chains shows that the standardized sum converges to a standard normal distribution as n→∞, independently of the starting state.
