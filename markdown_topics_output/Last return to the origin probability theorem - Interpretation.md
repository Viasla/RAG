# Interpretation
The formula gives the exact distribution of the time of the final zero of a symmetric random walk of even length. It tells us how likely it is that the walk will stay away from the origin for the last \(m-k\) pairs of steps.

Because the expression is symmetric in k and m-k, the distribution is symmetric around the midpoint \(k=m/2\). This symmetry underlies the result in Exercise 6(b) that, when m is odd, the probability of no equalization in the last m steps is 1/2.

The theorem links two classical combinatorial quantities: the central binomial coefficient (through u_{2k}) and the Catalan‑type count of paths that avoid zero (through u_{2m-2k}).
