# Intuition
Correlation measures how strongly two random variables move together; a positive correlation indicates that when one variable is above its mean, the other tends to be above its mean as well, while a negative correlation indicates opposite tendencies.

When X and Y are positively correlated (0<ρ<1) and Y exceeds its mean, the conditional expectation E(X|Y=y) is pulled toward ρy, which is less than y, demonstrating regression toward the mean.
