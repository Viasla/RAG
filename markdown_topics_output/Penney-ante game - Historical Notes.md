# Historical Notes
Penney‑ante was introduced by W. Penney in his 1969 paper “Problem: Penney‑Ante” in the Journal of Recreational Math. The game was later popularized by Martin Gardner in a 1974 Scientific American article, where John Conway’s formula for the odds was presented. An alternative combinatorial approach to pattern waiting times was given by Guibas and Odlyzko in 1981 (Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A).
