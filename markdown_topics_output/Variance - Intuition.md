# Intuition
Variance quantifies how much the possible values of a random variable are spread around its mean; a small variance indicates that outcomes are likely to be close to the expected value, whereas a large variance signals greater dispersion.

Because variance is the expected value of the squared deviation, it penalises larger departures more heavily, providing a sense of the typical magnitude of fluctuations around the mean.

Variance quantifies how far a random variable typically lies from its expected value, measuring the average squared distance \((X-\mu)^2\) from the mean \(\mu\).
