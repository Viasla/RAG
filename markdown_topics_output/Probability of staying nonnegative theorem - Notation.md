# Notation
\(S_k\): position of the random walk after \(k\) steps (with \(S_0=0\)).

\(u_{2m}\): the central binomial coefficient normalised by \(2^{2m}\), i.e., \(u_{2m}=\binom{2m}{m}/2^{2m}\).

\(P(\cdot)\): probability with respect to the uniform distribution over all \(2^{2m}\) possible walk sequences of length \(2m\).
