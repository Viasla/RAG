# Examples
1. Sweet pea seed experiment: seven weight groups each containing 10 seeds; each group’s offspring weights were normally distributed with variance identical to that of the parent group, yet the group means were closer to the overall mean weight than their respective parent group means.

2. Quincunx (Galton board) with a temporary stopping line AB: when the shot is released compartment by compartment, the resulting distribution at the bottom remains normal and shows that the intermediate stopping does not increase variance, implying a reversion effect towards the overall mean.
