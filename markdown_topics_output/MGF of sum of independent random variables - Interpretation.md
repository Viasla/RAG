# Interpretation
The product formula $g_{X+Y}(t)=g_X(t)g_Y(t)$ encapsulates the convolution of the probability mass functions $p_X$ and $p_Y$ into a simple algebraic multiplication. Consequently, the coefficients of the power‑series expansion of $g_{X+Y}(t)$ (or of the ordinary generating function $h_{X+Y}(z)$) directly yield the probability distribution of the sum.
