# Interpretation
The identity expresses a fundamental relationship between the total number of a‑shuffles of an n‑card deck and the Eulerian numbers that enumerate permutations by their number of rising sequences.  It shows that the weighted sum of Eulerian numbers, with weights given by binomial coefficients \binom{n+a-r}{n}, equals a^n.  This identity allows one to compute Eulerian numbers recursively: knowing all A(n,r) for r< a, one can solve for A(n,a).  Thus the theorem provides both a counting principle and a computational tool for Eulerian numbers.
