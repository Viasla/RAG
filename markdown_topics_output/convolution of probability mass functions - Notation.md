# Notation
$p_X$, $p_Y$, $p_Z$ denote the probability mass functions of $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ respectively; $p_X * p_Y$ denotes their convolution; $g_X(t)=E(e^{tX})$, $g_Y(t)$, $g_Z(t)$ are the moment generating functions; $h_X(z)=\sum_j p_X(j)z^j$, $h_Y(z)$, $h_Z(z)$ are the ordinary generating functions; $*$ is used as the convolution operator.
