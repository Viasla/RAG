# Properties
The normal distribution is symmetric about its mean and has bell‑shaped density; its total area equals 1.

The standardized normal distribution has mean 0 and variance 1, and its cumulative function N(x) gives probabilities P(Z ≤ x).

By the Central Limit Theorem, for large n the distribution of S_n* becomes arbitrarily close to N(x), enabling accurate probability approximations for sums of independent trials.
