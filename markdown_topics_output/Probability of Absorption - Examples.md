# Examples
In the Drunkard’s Walk example, the transition matrix in canonical form has absorbing states 4 and 5. The chain starts in a transient state (1, 2, or 3). By Theorem 11.3, regardless of the starting transient state, the probability that the drunkard eventually lands on either end of the walk (state 4 or 5) is 1; the process is absorbed almost surely.
