# Examples
The Land of Oz weather chain is ergodic; Theorem 11.10 guarantees its unique stationary distribution $w=(0.4,0.2,0.4)$, which is strictly positive and to which any initial distribution converges. In Example 11.22, the nine‑state maze is ergodic but not regular; the theorem still ensures a unique strictly positive stationary distribution for its transition matrix, and that any invariant row vector is a multiple of this distribution.
