# Proof
The proof is not given in the excerpt; it is stated that a version of this theorem for symmetric random walks can be found in Feller (pp. 93–94). The result follows from the arc‑sine distribution that appears in the limiting behavior of the last equalization time and the number of times the walk is above the t‑axis, and it extends those discrete results to the continuous‑distribution setting. The proof relies on the symmetry of F and the convergence of scaled probabilities to the density f(x)=1/(π√{x(1−x)}).
