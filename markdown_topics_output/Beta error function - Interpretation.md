# Interpretation
A small β(p) indicates that the test is powerful against the specific alternative value of p; it means the test will correctly reject the null hypothesis most of the time when that alternative is true.  By choosing m so that both α(.6) and β(0.8) are below a pre‑specified tolerance (e.g., 5 %), the experiment balances the risk of false positives and false negatives.  The choice of m between 69 and 73 reflects this balance: a larger m reduces α but increases β, while a smaller m reduces β but increases α.
