# Intuition
Theorem 11.2 encapsulates the idea that the distribution of states after n steps in a Markov chain is obtained by repeatedly applying the transition matrix to the initial distribution.  Intuitively, multiplying the initial probability vector u by P once gives the distribution after one step, multiplying by P again gives the distribution after two steps, and so forth, so that uPⁿ yields the distribution after n steps.  The i‑th component of the resulting vector is the probability of being in state s_i after n steps.
