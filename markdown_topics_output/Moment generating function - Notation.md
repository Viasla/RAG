# Notation
$g(t)$ – moment generating function of $X$ (also written $M_X(t)$ in some texts).

$h(z)$ – ordinary generating function, $h(z)=\sum_{j\ge0}p(j)z^j$, related by $h(z)=g(\log z)$.

$\mu_k$ – $k$‑th raw moment $E(X^k)$.

$p(j)$ – probability $P(X=j)$.

$X^*=(X-\mu)/\sigma$ – standardized variable.

$Y=X+a$ – shift of $X$ by constant $a$.

$Y=bX$ – scaling of $X$ by $b$.
