# Notation
m – mean number of offspring produced by a single parent (also written as E[offspring]).

m_n – mean number of offspring in the n‑th generation, satisfying m_n=m^n.

h(z) – probability‑generating function of the offspring distribution, h(z)=\sum p_k z^k.

h'(z) – derivative of h(z); h'(1)=m.

p_k – probability that a parent produces exactly k offspring.

p_0, p_1, p_2 – specific probabilities for 0, 1, 2 offspring in the two‑offspring model.
