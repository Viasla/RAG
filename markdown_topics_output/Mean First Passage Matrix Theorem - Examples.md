# Examples
In the “Land of Oz’’ example the transition matrix $P$ and stationary matrix $W$ are given, leading to
$$Z=\begin{pmatrix} 86/75 & 1/25 & -14/75\\ 2/25 & 21/25 & 2/25\\ -14/75 & 1/25 & 86/75\end{pmatrix},\qquad w=(2/5,\,1/5,\,2/5).$$
Applying the theorem, for instance
$$m_{12}=\frac{z_{22}-z_{12}}{w_2}=\frac{21/25-1/25}{1/5}=4,$$
and similarly computing all entries gives
$$M=\begin{pmatrix}0 & 4 & 10/3\\ 8/3 & 0 & 8/3\\ 10/3 & 4 & 0\end{pmatrix}.$$
