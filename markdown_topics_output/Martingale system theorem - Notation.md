# Notation
$T^B$ – random time until the first occurrence of pattern $B$.  $BB$ – total payout to gamblers who win when pattern $B$ occurs.  $AB$ – total amount gamblers win when they arrive during the occurrence of pattern $A$.  $E(cdot)$ – expected value operator.  The notation $E(T^B)=BB$ expresses the equality derived from the martingale property.
