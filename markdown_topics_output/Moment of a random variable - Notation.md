# Notation
(X) – random variable; (x_j) – values in its range; (p(x_j)=P(X=x_j)); (mu_k) – (k)th moment (E(X^k)); (mu=mu_1); (sigma^2=mu_2-mu_1^2); (g(t)=E(e^{tX})); (t) – real parameter; (p_X(j)) – pmf; (q=1-p) in binomial context; (p) and (q) used in geometric and binomial pmfs; (lambda) – mean of Poisson distribution.

μ_n denotes the n-th moment of X; g(t) denotes the moment generating function; X denotes the random variable; f_X(x) denotes its density function; E denotes expectation.
