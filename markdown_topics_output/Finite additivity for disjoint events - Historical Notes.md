# Historical Notes
Finite additivity for disjoint events is first introduced as Property 4 in Theorem 1.1, derived from the basic definition of a probability distribution $m$ on a sample space $\Omega$.  It states that if $A$ and $B$ are disjoint subsets of $\Omega$ then $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)$.  This property is later generalized in Theorem 1.2 to any finite collection of pairwise disjoint events, and its importance is highlighted by the fact that it is one of the fundamental axioms underlying all probability theory.
