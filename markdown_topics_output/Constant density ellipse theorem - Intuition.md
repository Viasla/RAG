# Intuition
The quadratic form x^2 - 2ρxy + y^2 captures how the probability mass of a bivariate normal is stretched and tilted in the plane. When ρ = 0 the cross term vanishes and the level curves are circles aligned with the coordinate axes. As ρ moves toward ±1 the ellipse becomes more elongated along the line y = ±x, reflecting stronger positive or negative linear dependence. The parametric equations describe this stretching by scaling the cosθ and sinθ components along orthogonal axes that are aligned with the principal axes of the ellipse.
