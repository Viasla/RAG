# Intuition
The inequality bounds the probability that the average of independent random variables deviates from its mean by a specified amount, using only the variance and without assuming any particular distribution.

Chebyshev’s inequality gives an upper bound for tail probabilities that depends only on the variance, independent of the distribution’s shape, and thus provides a conservative estimate. It is often used when the distribution is unknown or when a guarantee is required regardless of distribution. In the exercises, this conservative nature is illustrated by comparing it to the tighter normal approximation.
