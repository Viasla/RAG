# Historical Notes
Li used a theorem from martingale theory to argue that the expected amount the casino takes in equals the expected amount it pays out, leading to the formula E(T^B)=BB for the expected time to reach a pattern for the first time.

Guibas and Odlyzko gave an elegant combinatorial method based on counting sequences that avoid a pattern, deriving the identity \sum_{n=0}^{\infty}P(T>n)=2+8=10 for the pattern HTH and thus confirming the expected time of 10 tosses.

The gambling interpretation of Exercise 28 and 29 connects the expected time to reach a pattern with the fair‑game winnings of gamblers who bet on successive coin tosses.

The absorbing Markov chain approach treats the set of possible initial segments of a pattern as states, making the time to absorption equal to the expected time to first occurrence of the pattern.
