# Historical Notes
The notion of a rising sequence in a permutation was introduced in the study of riffle shuffles, where it was observed that the probability of obtaining a given ordering after an a‑shuffle depends only on the number of rising sequences of that ordering.

The count of permutations of {1,…,n} with a given number of rising sequences is given by the Eulerian numbers A(n,r), a classical combinatorial sequence first studied in the 18th century.

The notion of rising sequences is introduced in the context of analysing riffle shuffles, appearing in Exercise 1 where it is related to falls in the inverse permutation.
