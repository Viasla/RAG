# Intuition
The lemma expresses that if we have an infinite sequence of independent events whose individual probabilities do not become too small (in the sense that their sum is infinite), then almost surely we will observe infinitely many of them. Intuitively, each event contributes a non‑negligible amount of “weight” to the probability space, and the cumulative weight diverges, forcing an infinite number of successes.


In the example with random variables (X_i) the events (A_i={|X_i|ge 	frac12 i}) satisfy (sum P(A_i)=infty); by the lemma we conclude that such large deviations happen infinitely often with probability one.
