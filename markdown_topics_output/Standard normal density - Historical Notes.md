# Historical Notes
The standard normal density is introduced in Chapter 4.3 of the source text, where the general normal density function is defined and the special case with mean μ=0 and variance σ^2=1 is singled out and named φ(x).

It appears again in Chapter 9 as the limiting distribution in the Central Limit Theorem for Bernoulli trials, and the text discusses its use in fitting the height‑corrected spike graphs of standardized binomial sums.
