# Properties
If A and B are disjoint subsets of the sample space Ω, then P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B) (Property 4 of Theorem 1.1).

For general (possibly overlapping) subsets A and B, P(A∪B)=P(A)+P(B)−P(A∩B) (Theorem 1.4, equation (1.1)).

Theorem 1.2 states that for pairwise disjoint events A1,…,An, the probability of their union equals the sum of the individual probabilities: P(A1∪⋯∪An)=∑iP(Ai).

The complement of a set A satisfies A∪Ã=Ω, which implies P(A∪Ã)=1 by Property 2.
