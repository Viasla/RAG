# Proof
The derivation of \(L=\sqrt{2-2\cos\alpha}\) follows directly from the Law of Cosines applied to a triangle formed by two radii of the unit circle and the chord connecting their endpoints. The two radii are of length 1, the included angle between them is \(\alpha\), and the chord is the third side. Substituting \(a=b=1\) and \(\gamma=\alpha\) into the law \(c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos\gamma\) gives \(c^2=1^2+1^2-2(1)(1)\cos\alpha=2-2\cos\alpha\), from which \(L=c=\sqrt{2-2\cos\alpha}\).
