# Notation
Intersection of events is denoted by ∩; the complement of event Ai is denoted by ~Ai. The product of probabilities is written as P(A1)P(A2)…P(An).

(P(Emid F)) denotes the conditional probability of (E) given (F); (P(Ecap F)) denotes the probability of both events occurring simultaneously.  Independence is often expressed succinctly as (P(Ecap F)=P(E)P(F)).  The notation (Eperp F) may also be used to indicate that (E) and (F) are independent.

Independence is expressed as (Pr(Acap B)=Pr(A)Pr(B)).  In product notation, (Pr(Acap B)=Pr(A),Pr(B)).  Conditional independence is written (Pr(A|B)=Pr(A)).
