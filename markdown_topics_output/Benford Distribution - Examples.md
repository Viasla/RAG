# Examples
In President Clinton’s tax‑return data, the empirical frequencies of the digits 1 through 9 align closely with the probabilities given by the Benford formula, demonstrating a real‑world fit of the law.

Forensic accountants, following Nigrini’s methodology, compute the observed leading‑digit frequencies of a set of financial transactions and compare them to the Benford probabilities; a significant deviation may signal manipulation.
