# Interpretation
The definition says that observing the whole vector \((X_1,\dots,X_n)\) is equivalent to observing each coordinate separately, because the joint probability of any particular combination \((r_1,\dots,r_n)\) factors as the product of the individual probabilities. Thus the joint distribution of the process is simply the product of the identical marginal distributions, reflecting both independence and identicalness of the trials.

$S_n$ represents the cumulative outcome after $n$ independent trials, while $A_n$ is the average outcome per trial; $S_n^*$ rescales $S_n$ to have mean 0 and variance 1, providing a standardized version useful for limit theorems.
