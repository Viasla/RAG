# Properties
The total number of possible outcomes for a staged experiment equals the product of the number of choices at each stage (\(N = n_1 \cdot n_2 \cdot \dots \cdot n_r\)).

For probabilistic trees, the probability of any complete outcome is the product of the probabilities on the branches leading to that outcome.

The probabilities assigned to all leaf nodes of a tree diagram sum to 1, ensuring a valid probability model.

Tree diagrams facilitate the computation of joint probabilities and the visualization of conditional relationships between stages.
