# Properties
The probabilities sum to one: \sum_{k=1}^{9}f(k)=1.

The distribution is strictly decreasing in k, making digit 1 the most common leading digit and digit 9 the least common.

It is the unique distribution that is invariant under multiplication of all data by a positive constant (scale invariance).

The Benford law applies to data that span several orders of magnitude and are not confined to a narrow range or artificially truncated.

When applied to fabricated or uniformly generated numbers, the observed digit frequencies typically diverge from the Benford probabilities, providing a basis for statistical fraud detection.
