# Notation
The underlying single‑experiment sample space is denoted \(R=\{r_1,r_2,\dots,r_s\}\) with distribution matrix \(m_X=\begin{pmatrix}r_1&\dots&r_s\\ p_1&\dots&p_s\end{pmatrix}\). The repeated experiment uses the product space \(\Omega=R\times R\times\dots\times R=R^n\). For a specific outcome \(\omega=(\omega_1,\dots,\omega_n)\) the product distribution is \(m(\omega)=m(\omega_1)\cdots m(\omega_n)\). The random variables \(X_j\) are defined as the \(j\)th coordinate of \(\omega\). The vector \(\mathbf X=(X_1,\dots,X_n)\) denotes the joint random variable of the process.

The individual trials are denoted by $X_1,X_2,\dots ,X_n$; the total after $n$ trials is $S_n=\sum_{i=1}^n X_i$; the sample mean is $A_n=S_n/n$; the standardized total is $S_n^*=(S_n-n\mu)/\sqrt{n\sigma^2}$.
