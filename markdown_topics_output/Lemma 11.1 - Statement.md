# Statement
Let P be an r‑by‑r transition matrix with no zero entries. Let d be the smallest entry of the matrix. Let y be a column vector with r components, the largest of which is M0 and the smallest m0. Let M1 and m1 be the largest and smallest component, respectively, of the vector Py. Then M1 - m1 ≤ (1 - 2d)(M0 - m0).
