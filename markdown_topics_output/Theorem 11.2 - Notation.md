# Notation
u – probability vector that represents the starting distribution of states; each entry u_i is the probability that the chain starts in state s_i.  P – transition matrix of the Markov chain; P_{ij} is the probability of transitioning from state s_i to state s_j in one step.  Pⁿ – n-th power of the transition matrix, representing the combined transition probabilities over n successive steps.  u^(n) – probability vector after n steps, defined by u^(n)=uPⁿ; the i‑th entry of u^(n) is the probability that the chain is in state s_i after n steps.
