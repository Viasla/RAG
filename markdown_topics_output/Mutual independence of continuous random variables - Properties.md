# Properties
If X1,…,Xn are mutually independent, the joint distribution factorises: the joint CDF is the product of the marginal CDFs and the joint density is the product of the marginal densities.

Mutual independence is equivalent to pairwise independence for the whole collection.

Theorem 4.3: Any continuous functions of independent continuous random variables are themselves mutually independent.

Definition 4.8: A sequence of mutually independent and identically distributed continuous random variables is called an independent trials process.
