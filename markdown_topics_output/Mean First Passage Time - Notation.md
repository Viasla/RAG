# Notation
The symbol $m_{ij}$ denotes the mean first passage time from state $s_i$ to state $s_j$ (with $m_{ii}=0$).

The matrix $mathbf{M}$ has entries $m_{ij}$ for $i
eq j$ and zeros on the diagonal; it is called the *mean first passage matrix*.

The vector $mathbf{c}$ is the column vector of all ones used in the product $mathbf{N}mathbf{c}$ to obtain expected absorption times.

m_{ij} – mean first passage time from state i to state j;  Z – fundamental matrix (Z=(I-P+W)^{-1});  w – stationary (fixed) probability vector of the chain;  r_i – mean recurrence time for state i (often equal to 1/w_i);  \bar{m}_i – mean time until the next occurrence of state i when the chain is in equilibrium;  K – Kemeny constant, defined as \sum_j z_{jj}-1.
