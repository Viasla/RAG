# Interpretation
The coefficient of $z^j$ in the power‑series expansion of $h(z)$ equals $p_j$, the probability of selling exactly $j$ letters; thus $h(z)$ compactly encodes the entire Poisson probability mass function.

Because $h(z)=e^{m(z-1)}$, evaluating derivatives at $z=1$ yields moments of the distribution: the first derivative gives the mean $m$, and higher derivatives give higher moments.
