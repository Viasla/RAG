# Proof
The proof observes that if an outcome ω belongs to exactly k of the sets, it is counted k times in the first sum, subtracted C(k,2) times in the second, added C(k,3) times in the third, etc. Thus its total count is C(k,1)−C(k,2)+C(k,3)−…+(-1)^{k-1}C(k,k), which equals 1, as follows from the binomial expansion of (1−1)^k =0. Therefore each outcome’s probability is added exactly once on the right-hand side, matching the left-hand side, proving the equality.
