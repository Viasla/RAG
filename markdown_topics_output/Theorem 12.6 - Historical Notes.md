# Historical Notes
This result generalizes earlier arc‑sine laws for random walks, such as Theorem 12.4 (probability of being in the lead) and Theorem 12.5 (smallest subscript with S_j=S_{2m}). It is attributed to Feller, who presents a similar statement for symmetric random walks in the cited pages. The theorem illustrates the widespread appearance of the arcsine density in various properties of random walks.
