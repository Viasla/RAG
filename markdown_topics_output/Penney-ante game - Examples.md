# Examples
If A=HT and B=HTH, the gambler who arrives while A is being formed would win $2 on the first H and $2 on the subsequent T, then lose on the next bet, giving AB=4. The total winnings if B occurs are BB=10 (2+4+8). Hence E_A(T^B)=BB-AB=6 tosses on average starting from A. For A=HH and B=HTH, AB=2 (win on the first H then lose), while BB remains 10, so E_A(T^B)=8. When A=B=HTH, AB=BB=10, reflecting that the gambler wins the full sequence of bets (2+4+8).
