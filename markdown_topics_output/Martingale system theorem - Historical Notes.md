# Historical Notes
The martingale system theorem is rooted in the theory of martingales developed in probability theory in the early 20th century.  Its application to gambling was popularized in the 1960s and 1970s, notably in Penney’s work on non‑transitive games and in later analyses of pattern‑matching problems such as those presented by Li.  The theorem provides a rigorous foundation for the intuitive “fairness” of games where each bet is a zero‑expected‑value gamble.
