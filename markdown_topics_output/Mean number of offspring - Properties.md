# Properties
If m\le1 then the extinction probability d equals 1; if m>1 the extinction probability is the unique root d<1 of z=h(z).

The mean number of offspring in successive generations satisfies the recursion m_{n+1}=m_n\cdot m, yielding the closed form m_n=m^n.

For the two‑offspring model the mean can be expressed as m=1-p_0+p_2, showing a direct relationship between the probabilities of zero and two offspring and the expected reproductive output.

In the geometric offspring distribution, the mean m=\frac{b}{(1-c)^2} shows how the parameters b and c control the expected number of children and thus the extinction behavior.
