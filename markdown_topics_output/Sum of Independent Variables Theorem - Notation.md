# Notation
f_X(x) – density of random variable X.

f_Y(y) – density of random variable Y.

f_Z(z) – density of the sum Z = X + Y.

Convolution of two functions f and g:

(f * g)(z) = \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} f(z - y) g(y) \,dy.

In the theorem, f_X * f_Y denotes the convolution of the two densities.
