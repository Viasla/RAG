# Notation
Random numbers can be generated by the von Neumann middle‐digit squaring method: choose a seed X_0, compute X_{n+1}=middle digits of X_n^2; or by a linear congruential generator using the formula X_{n+1} = (aX_n + c) mod m, where a, c, and m are constants; dividing each X_j by m yields a sequence of pseudorandom numbers in the interval [0,1).
