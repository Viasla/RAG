# Proof
The proof follows from the ballot‑type argument that a walk ending at height $r$ and never falling below zero must have the same number of up‑steps as down‑steps except for the excess $r$; counting such paths yields $p_{n,r}$ when the parity matches, and $p_{n,r+1}$ otherwise. A detailed combinatorial derivation is given in Feller (1968), Example 12.3.
