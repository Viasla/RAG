# Notation
m_{ij} denotes the mean first passage time from state s_i to state s_j.  r_i denotes the mean recurrence time of state s_i.  w_i denotes the i-th component of the stationary (fixed probability) vector of the transition matrix P.  M is the mean first passage matrix.  D is the diagonal matrix of mean recurrence times.  C is the all‑ones matrix.  P is the transition matrix.  I is the identity matrix.

$P$ denotes the transition matrix of the Markov chain.

$Z$ denotes the fundamental matrix of the chain, defined as $Z=(I-P+W)^{-1}$ where $W$ is the rank–1 matrix with all rows equal to the stationary distribution $w$.

$w$ denotes the stationary distribution (fixed vector) of an ergodic chain.

$m_{ij}$ denotes the $(i,j)$ entry of the mean first passage matrix, i.e. the expected number of steps to reach state $j$ for the first time when starting from state $i$.
