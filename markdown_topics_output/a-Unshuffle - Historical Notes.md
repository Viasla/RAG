# Historical Notes
The notion of an a-unshuffle was introduced in the study of riffle shuffles and is discussed in the proof of Theorem 3.9, where a coding of the set of a‑unshuffles is presented.

The coding of a‑unshuffles parallels the coding of a‑shuffles and has been used to analyse the mixing time of repeated shuffling processes, such as the 2‑unshuffle (riffle) used in card‑shuffling experiments.
