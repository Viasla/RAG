# Properties
If $X$ and $Y$ are independent, then the mgf of their sum is the product of the individual mgfs: $g_{X+Y}(t)=g_X(t)g_Y(t)$.

Similarly, the ordinary generating function of the sum satisfies $h_{X+Y}(z)=h_X(z)h_Y(z)$.

Consequently, convolution of pmfs translates into multiplication of generating functions, turning a potentially complicated summation into a simple algebraic product.

The convolution operation is associative and commutative, inherited from the commutative multiplication of generating functions.

The examples show that specific families of distributions are closed under convolution: binomial$(n,p)$ is closed (parameters add), and geometric distributions convolve to a negative‑binomial distribution.
