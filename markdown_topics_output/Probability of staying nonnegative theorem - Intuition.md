# Intuition
A walk that never returns to the origin must move away from zero on the first step; by symmetry half of those walks begin with a +1 and therefore stay at or above zero for all subsequent steps.

The quantity \(u_{2m}\) also counts the proportion of Dyck‑type paths (Catalan‑related objects) among all \(2m\)-step lattice paths, reflecting the combinatorial nature of the non‑negativity constraint.
