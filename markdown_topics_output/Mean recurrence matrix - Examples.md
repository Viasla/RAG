# Examples
In the maze example (Example 11.22), the stationary distribution is w = (1/12, 1/8, 1/12, 1/8, 1/6, 1/8, 1/12, 1/8, 1/12).  The reciprocals give the mean recurrence times r = (12, 8, 12, 8, 6, 8, 12, 8, 12), so the diagonal of D is (12, 8, 12, 8, 6, 8, 12, 8, 12).  In the Land of Oz weather example, the stationary vector is w = (2/5, 1/5, 2/5), leading to mean recurrence times r = (5/2, 5, 5/2) and hence the diagonal of D is (5/2, 5, 5/2).
