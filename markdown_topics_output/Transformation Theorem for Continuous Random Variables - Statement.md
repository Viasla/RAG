# Statement
Let X be a random variable with cumulative distribution function F_X strictly increasing on the range of X.  Let Y = F_X(X).  Then Y is uniformly distributed on the interval [0,1].

In Exercise 18 the cumulative distribution functions of the linear transforms Y = X + b , Z = aX , and W = aX + b (with constants a and b) are to be determined, indicating how linear transformations affect the CDF.

In Exercise 19 the density functions of the same linear transforms Y, Z, and W (with a ≠ 0) are to be derived, showing the effect on the PDF.

In Exercise 20 the random variable X is uniformly distributed on [c,d] and the problem asks for the choice of a and b such that Y = aX + b is uniformly distributed on [0,1].
