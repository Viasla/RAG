# Notation
$m$ – mean (expected) number of offspring produced by a single parent; $m_n$ – mean number of individuals in the $n$‑th generation; $h(z)$ – probability generating function of the offspring distribution; $h'(1)$ – derivative of $h$ at $z=1$, equal to $m$; $h_n(z)$ – generating function of the population size in generation $n$.
