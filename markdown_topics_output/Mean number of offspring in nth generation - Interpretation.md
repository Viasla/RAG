# Interpretation
$m_n$ is the expected size of the $n$th generation of a Galton–Watson branching process. When $m>1$, $m_n$ grows without bound (exponential growth), indicating a supercritical process; when $m<1$, $m_n$ declines to zero, indicating a subcritical process that will almost surely become extinct; when $m=1$, $m_n$ remains constant, characterising a critical process where extinction occurs with probability one but the mean size does not explode.
