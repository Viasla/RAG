# Intuition
The joint cumulative distribution function extends the idea of a univariate cumulative distribution to a vector of random variables, representing the probability that each variable falls below a specified threshold simultaneously. It accumulates probability over an n‑dimensional rectangle defined by the lower limits (-infty) and the thresholds (x_1,dots,x_n).

When the underlying random variables are independent, the joint cumulative distribution function reduces to the product of the individual cumulative distribution functions, illustrating how independence simplifies the multivariate probability structure.
