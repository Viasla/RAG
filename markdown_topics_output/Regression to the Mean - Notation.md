# Notation
The term ‘regression to the mean’ refers to the linear relationship between parental deviation from the population mean (ΔP) and progeny deviation (ΔO), expressed as ΔO = r·ΔP where 0 < r < 1. Galton’s original estimate used r = 1/3, indicating that the offspring’s mean deviation from the population mean was one third that of the parents.
