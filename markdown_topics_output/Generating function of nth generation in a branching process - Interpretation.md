# Interpretation
h_n(z) is the expectation E(z^{Z_n}), so evaluating its coefficients yields P(Z_n=k) for each k. Setting z=1 gives h_n(1)=1, confirming a proper probability distribution.

The fixed‑point equation z=h(z) determines the probability d that the process eventually dies out; the smallest solution in [0,1] is the extinction probability.

In the geometric‑offspring case, h(z) is a rational function, and the composition h_n(z)=h^{\circ n}(z) remains rational, allowing explicit calculation of the entire distribution for any n.
