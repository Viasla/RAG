# Interpretation
The matrix $Z$ encapsulates the cumulative transient dynamics of an ergodic chain after removing the steady‑state component $W$. It can be used to compute mean first‑passage times more conveniently than solving the linear system $m_{ij}=1+\sum_{k\neq i}p_{ik}m_{kj}$. The text also indicates that $Z$ will appear in the statement of the Central Limit Theorem for Markov chains, underscoring its importance in asymptotic variance calculations.

The fundamental matrices N and Z are introduced to avoid eigenvalue calculations and to facilitate the computation of mean first passage times and limiting variances in Markov chain analysis.
