# Examples
Example 10.5 in the text: X and Y are independent with range {0,…,n} and binomial distribution p_X(j)=p_Y(j)=inom{n}{j}p^jq^{n-j}. The generating functions give g_X(t)=g_Y(t)=(p,e^t+q)^n and h_X(z)=h_Y(z)=(p,z+q)^n. The product yields g_Z(t)=(p,e^t+q)^{2n} and h_Z(z)=(p,z+q)^{2n}. Expanding h_Z(z) gives p_Z(j)=inom{2n}{j}p^jq^{2n-j}, the pmf of a Bin(2n,p) distribution.
