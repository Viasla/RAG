# Interpretation
p_{ij} gives the probability that, given the chain is in state i today, it will be in state j tomorrow; p_{ij}^{(n)} gives the probability that the chain will be in state j after n steps starting from i.

The entry (i,j) of \mathbf{P}^n can be interpreted as the probability of a specific sequence of n−1 intermediate states that connects i to j, summed over all possible intermediate states.
