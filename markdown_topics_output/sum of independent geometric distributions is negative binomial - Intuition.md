# Intuition
A geometric random variable counts the number of failures before the first success in a sequence of Bernoulli trials. Summing two such independent counts is equivalent to counting failures before the second success in a sequence of trials, which is exactly what a negative binomial distribution models. The generating‑function calculation confirms this intuition by producing a coefficient that grows linearly with j, the familiar {j+1} factor of a NB(2,p) distribution.
