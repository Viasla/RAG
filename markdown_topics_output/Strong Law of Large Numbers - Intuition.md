# Intuition
No intuition or conceptual explanation of the Strong Law of Large Numbers is given in this excerpt; the text focuses on the weak form and its relation to frequency interpretation, noting that the strong law is a different theorem that will be presented separately.

SLLN guarantees that the empirical average of independent identically distributed random variables converges to the expected value for almost every infinite outcome sequence.

This means that in the long run the proportion of heads in coin tosses will be exactly 1/2 with probability one.
