# Intuition
Simulation reproduces an experiment’s outcome distribution by performing a large number of independent trials, thereby allowing empirical estimation of probabilities and testing of theoretical models.

Repeated random experiments, even if generated by deterministic pseudo‑random algorithms, emulate the behavior of truly random processes when the seed is varied.

Monte Carlo methods rely on the law of large numbers: as the number of simulated repetitions increases, the observed frequencies approach the theoretical probabilities.
