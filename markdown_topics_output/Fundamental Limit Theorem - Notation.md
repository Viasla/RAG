# Notation
$\mathbf{P}$ – transition matrix of a regular Markov chain;\n$\mathbf{W}$ – limit of $\mathbf{P}^{n}$, a matrix with identical rows equal to the unique fixed probability row vector $\mathbf{w}$;\n$\mathbf{w}$ – stationary distribution (row vector) satisfying $\mathbf{w}\mathbf{P}=\mathbf{w}$;\n$M_{n}$ – maximum component of $\mathbf{P}^{n}\mathbf{y}$;\n$m_{n}$ – minimum component of $\mathbf{P}^{n}\mathbf{y}$;\n$d$ – smallest entry of $\mathbf{P}$;\n$r$ – number of states;\n$\mathbf{y}$ – arbitrary column vector;\n$\mathbf{I}$ – identity matrix.
