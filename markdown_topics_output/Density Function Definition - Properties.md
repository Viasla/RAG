# Properties
f(x)\ge0 for all x (non‑negativity).

\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx=1, i.e., the total area under the density equals one (implicit in the definition of a probability measure).

If f is constant on a region, the corresponding distribution is uniform (equiprobable) on that region.

The probability of an interval depends on both its length and the location of the interval when f is not constant, as illustrated by the radial density where P([a,b])=2(b-a)\cdot\frac{a+b}{2}.

The density may be zero outside a specified support, restricting the random variable to that support.
