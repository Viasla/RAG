# Intuition
The cumulative distribution function measures the total probability mass up to a point; the density function measures the instantaneous rate of probability. The integral of the density up to (x) accumulates the probability mass, giving the CDF.

Because the CDF is obtained by integrating the density, the density is its derivative; thus the graph of the CDF is the antiderivative of the density, and points where the density is large correspond to steep increases in the CDF.
