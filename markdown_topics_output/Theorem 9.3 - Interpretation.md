# Interpretation
Theorem 9.3 demonstrates that even for integer‑valued bounded random variables, the properly normalized sum behaves asymptotically like a continuous normal variable.  The step‑function extension provides a bridge between discrete probability masses and continuous density functions, enabling the application of characteristic‑function arguments.  The theorem’s conclusion—that the distribution functions converge to the standard normal distribution and that the densities converge to the Gaussian density—confirms the universality of the normal law in the CLT setting.
