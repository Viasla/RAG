# Notation
$p_j=\frac{e^{-m}m^j}{j!}$ denotes the probability that an individual sells $j$ letters under the Poisson model.

$h(z)$ denotes the probability‑generating function of the Poisson distribution.

$m$ denotes the mean (expected) number of letters sold by a single individual; it equals $Np$ in the binomial‑to‑Poisson approximation.

$z$ is the indeterminate of the generating function, whose coefficients of $z^j$ give $p_j$.
