# Notation
p_A – probability that pattern A occurs before pattern B;
 p_B – probability that pattern B occurs before pattern A (p_B = 1 – p_A);
 AA – expected waiting time (or “pay‑off”) for pattern A when played alone;
 BB – expected waiting time for pattern B when played alone;
 AB – expected amount earned by a gambler who arrives during an occurrence of A and then waits until B occurs;
 BA – analogous quantity when the gambler arrives during B and waits for A;
 E(T^{A or B}) – expected number of tosses until either A or B appears for the first time.
