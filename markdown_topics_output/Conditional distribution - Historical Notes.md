# Historical Notes
The notion of conditioning arose in the 17th century with the problem of points, solved by Fermat and Pascal.  Their work on dividing stakes when a game is unfinished introduced the idea of renormalising probabilities after some information is known, a precursor to the modern conditional distribution.

Later probability theory formalised this idea as a conditional distribution given an event with positive probability, as stated explicitly in problem 48 of the text.
