# Examples
If X_1,X_2,…,X_n are independent exponential(λ) random variables, then S_n=X_1+\dots+X_n has the gamma density g_n(x) given above; this models the total time until the nth emission in a radioactive source.

In a queueing model where inter‑arrival times are exponential, the time until the nth customer arrives follows a gamma distribution, providing a concrete application of the density.
