# Historical Notes
The result is sometimes referred to as the "First Fundamental Mystery of Probability," emphasizing that the additivity of expectations predates the formal development of measure‑theoretic probability.

While the theorem appears in many early probability texts, its explicit statement as Theorem 6.2 in the source reflects a pedagogical tradition of deriving it from the more general expectation‑of‑a‑function formula (Theorem 6.1).
