# Interpretation
Conway’s formula translates the combinatorial structure of the two patterns into a simple odds ratio. The numerator (BB – BA) measures how much longer it typically takes to see B after a B‑start compared to after an A‑start, while the denominator (AA – AB) measures the analogous difference for A. A larger numerator relative to the denominator means pattern A is less likely to win, giving the second player a strategic advantage. The formula thus provides an immediate way to compute optimal strategies in Penney‑ante without solving full Markov‑chain equations.
