# Notation
The notation used for a random walk includes X_k for the kth independent increment, S_n for the nth partial sum (position after n steps), and \mathbf{R}^m for the m‑dimensional Euclidean space in which the walk may take place.

The jump made at step n is expressed as S_n - S_{n-1} = X_n.

When the walk is confined to the real line (m=1) the increment distribution often takes the form f_X(x)=\tfrac12 for x=\pm1 and 0 otherwise.
