# Interpretation
The a‑shuffle model provides a probabilistic description of a generalized riffle process: each of the a^n possible labelings of the deck is equally likely, so the resulting permutation is uniformly chosen from the a‑shuffle distribution. The formula for the probability of r rising sequences shows how the a‑shuffle biases the permutation toward having few rising sequences when a is small, which in turn governs measures such as variation distance from the uniform distribution.

The expression for the location of the first card gives a concrete way to compute marginal probabilities of card positions under the a‑shuffle, illustrating how the shuffle spreads the top card across the deck with a distribution that depends on a and the deck size.
