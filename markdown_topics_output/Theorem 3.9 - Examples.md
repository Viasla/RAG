# Examples
An explicit example is given for a=2 and b=2. Figure 3.11 shows a 2‑unshuffle of a ten‑card deck, producing stacks of sizes 4 and 6 that recombine to the identity ordering. Figure 3.12 displays the corresponding 4‑unshuffle (the same as performing two successive 2‑unshuffles). Sorting the cards first by the second coordinate yields the two 2‑unshuffles, and sorting by the combined lexicographic label yields the 4‑unshuffle, both of which return the deck to the identity ordering, illustrating the theorem’s claim that two successive 2‑shuffles are equivalent to a single 4‑shuffle. The text also notes that s successive riffle shuffles are equivalent to a single 2^{s}‑shuffle, a direct corollary of Theorem 3.9.

For n = 6 and a = 3, let M = (1,0,2,2,0,2). Then n_0 = 2, n_1 = 1, n_2 = 3. Cards 1 and 2 receive label 0, card 3 receives label 1, and cards 4,5,6 receive label 2. Placing the labeled cards into the positions indicated by M yields the permutation (3,1,4,5,2,6).
