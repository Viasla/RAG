# Historical Notes
The exponential growth property of the mean number of offspring in a branching process is derived from the classical work of Galton and Watson on extinction probabilities, where the generating function technique was introduced to study the evolution of the process across generations.

The analysis presented here follows the exposition in the cited text, where the generating function of the offspring distribution, $h(z)$, and its derivative at 1, $h'(1)$, are used to obtain the recursive mean formula $m_{n+1}=m_n,m$, leading to $m_n=m^n$ for supercritical processes.
