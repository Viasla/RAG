# Intuition
The memoryless property means that the probability of waiting an additional s units of time does not depend on how much time has already elapsed; future is independent of past.

The memoryless property expresses that, for certain random variables, the probability of surviving an additional time s after having survived time r depends only on s and not on r. This means the future evolution of the process does not depend on how long it has already run. This intuitive idea is why exponential and geometric distributions are called memoryless.
