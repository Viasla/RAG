# Notation
S_k: position of the simple symmetric random walk after k steps (S_0=0).

u_{2n}=\frac{\binom{2n}{n}}{2^{2n}}: probability that a walk of length 2n returns to the origin at its final step.

\(\binom{a}{b}\): binomial coefficient, zero when b<0 or b>a.

Last return to the origin at time 2k: the greatest even time \le 2m at which S_{2k}=0.

m: half the total length of the walk; k: half the time of the last return.
