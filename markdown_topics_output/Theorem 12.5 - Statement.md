# Statement
Theorem 12.5 states that for a symmetric random walk of length $2m$, let $J$ be the smallest even index $2k$ such that the walk returns to its final value, i.e. $S_j=S_{2m}$.  Define $gamma_{2k,2m}$ as the probability that $J=2k$.  Then $gamma_{2k,2m}$ equals the probability $alpha_{2k,2m}$ that the last equalization of the walk occurs at time $2k$.  Explicitly, $gamma_{2k,2m}=alpha_{2k,2m}$ for all $k=0,1,dots,m$.

Theorem 12.5 is referenced in the text, but its explicit statement is not included in the excerpt. The text merely indicates that part (c) will use the results of parts (a) and (b) to prove it.
