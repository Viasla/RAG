# Notation
T^B – the random time (number of tosses) until pattern B first occurs.

BB – the total amount paid to gamblers who win when pattern B finally appears (the “payout” associated with B).

AB – the total amount earned by gamblers who arrived while pattern A was present and subsequently bet on B.

E(T^B) – the expected value of T^B, i.e., the expected waiting time for pattern B.

E_A(T^B) – the expected additional waiting time for B given that the current suffix equals pattern A.

f(n) – the number of length‑n sequences that avoid a given pattern.

f_p(n) – the number of length‑n sequences that obtain the pattern for the first time at toss n.
