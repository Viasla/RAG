The Labouchere system is a strategy for betting on a binary outcome (e.g., red vs. black in roulette) that attempts to guarantee a fixed net profit equal to the sum of the original list. It dynamically changes bet size based on previous wins and losses, and the rule that a win removes two numbers while a loss adds a new number is intended to balance risk and reward.
