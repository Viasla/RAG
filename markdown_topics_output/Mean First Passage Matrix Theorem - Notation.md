# Notation
$P$ – transition matrix of the ergodic chain;
$W$ – matrix whose rows are the stationary row vector $w$;
$w$ – stationary row probability vector satisfying $wP=w$ and $wW=w$;
$I$ – identity matrix;
$Z=(I-P+W)^{-1}$ – fundamental matrix;
$c$ – column vector of all ones;
$M$ – mean first‑passage matrix with entries $m_{ij}$;
$z_{ij}$ – $(i,j)$ entry of $Z$;
$r_j=1/w_j$ – reciprocal of the $j$‑th stationary probability.
