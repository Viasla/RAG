# Historical Notes
The Mean First Passage Matrix Theorem appears in Section 11.5 of a standard Markov‑chain textbook, following the development of the fundamental matrix $Z=(I-P+W)^{-1}$ for ergodic chains. It is presented as Theorem 11.16 and builds on earlier lemmas concerning the properties of $Z$, $W$, and the stationary row vector $w$.
