# Intuition
The conditional expected value E(X|Y=y) represents the average value of X one would observe if the outcome of Y were fixed at y; it captures how knowledge of Y influences the anticipated value of X.

When X and Y are positively correlated, the conditional expectation tends to move towards the overall mean, explaining the phenomenon that unusually tall fathers tend to have sons whose heights are, on average, closer to the population mean (regression toward the mean).
