# Formal Definition
Let A and B be two distinct binary strings of H (heads) and T (tails) with the property that neither is a subpattern of the other. A fair coin is tossed repeatedly until either A or B appears as a contiguous block; the player whose pattern appears first wins. Denote by p_A the probability that A appears before B and by p_B=1-p_A the probability that B appears first. Define E(T^B) as the expected number of tosses until pattern B appears when starting from scratch, and E_A(T^B) as the expected additional tosses needed to reach B given that the initial sequence already matches A. The gambling interpretation yields the identities E(T^B)=BB and E_A(T^B)=BB-AB, where AA, BB, AB, and BA are the total winnings a gambler would collect when betting on successive symbols of the respective patterns.
