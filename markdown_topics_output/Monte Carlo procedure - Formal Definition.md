# Formal Definition
A Monte Carlo procedure estimates the measure of a set E (such as an area or a probability) by generating n independent uniformly distributed random points in a known superset (e.g., the unit square) and computing the estimator \(\hat p = \frac{\text{number of points in }E}{n}\); the estimate of the measure of E is then \(\hat p\times \text{measure of the superset}\).
