# Properties
Support: x>0; g_n(x)=0 for x\le 0.

Normalization: \int_0^{\infty} g_n(x)dx=1 for all n and λ.

When n=1 the gamma density reduces to the exponential density λe^{-\lambda x}.

The CDF is G_n(x)=1-e^{-\lambda x}\left(1+\frac{\lambda x}{1!}+\dots+\frac{(\lambda x)^{n-1}}{(n-1)!}\right) for x>0.

g_n(x) is the n‑fold convolution of the exponential density, reflecting the additive property of independent waiting times.

The gamma density is related to the Poisson distribution: P(Y=n)=e^{-\lambda t}(\lambda t)^n/n! arises from the probability that S_n\le t<S_{n+1}.
