# Intuition
The axioms describe basic properties that any probability distribution must satisfy: non‑negativity, normalization to one, monotonicity with respect to inclusion, finite additivity for disjoint events, and a complement rule. The text explains that these properties follow from the definition of probability as a sum of a non‑negative distribution function m over elementary outcomes. The axioms provide a foundation for further results such as inclusion–exclusion and the law of total probability.
