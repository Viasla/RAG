# Formal Definition
For a discrete offspring distribution with probabilities p_k, the generating function is h(z)=\sum_{k=0}^∞ p_k z^k. The mean number of offspring is the first derivative of the generating function at z=1: m=h'(1).

In the special case where a parent can produce at most two offspring, the mean simplifies to m=p_1+2p_2=1-p_0+p_2.

For a geometric offspring distribution p_k=b c^{k-1} (k\ge1) with parameters 0<b\le1-c, 0<c<1, the mean is m=\frac{b}{(1-c)^2}.
