# Intuition
Linearity of expectation says that the average outcome of a sum of random quantities is just the sum of their individual averages, because expectation is a weighted average and weights (probabilities) sum to one. The result holds regardless of any dependence between the variables, since the averaging process distributes over addition.

The theorem allows complex expectations to be broken into simpler pieces: by representing a random variable as a sum of indicators, one can compute its expectation by adding the probabilities of the underlying elementary events.
