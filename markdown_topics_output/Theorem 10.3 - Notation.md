# Notation
X – continuous random variable with density f_X(x);  μₖ – the k‑th moment, μₖ=E[X^k] = ∫ x^k f_X(x) dx;  g(t) – moment generating function defined by the power series g(t)=∑_{k≥0} μₖ t^k/k!;  M – non‑negative bound such that the support of X lies in [−M,M];  f_X(x) – density function of X.
