# Notation
ρ denotes the correlation coefficient between the two standardized normal variables; (x, y) are the coordinates in the joint density plot; f_{X,Y}(x,y) is the joint density function; C is a fixed density level; D is the constant that appears on the right‑hand side of the ellipse equation after solving for the exponent; r is a positive scalar controlling the size of the ellipse in the parametric representation; θ is the parametric angle varying from 0 to 2π.
