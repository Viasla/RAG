# Intuition
Since the left nullspace of P‑I is one‑dimensional, any two left eigenvectors associated with eigenvalue 1 must be scalar multiples of each other; normalizing to sum to 1 yields a unique probability vector, giving a clear geometric picture of a single direction in the row‑space that remains invariant under the transition dynamics.

The theorem reflects that in an ergodic (irreducible and aperiodic) Markov chain every state communicates with every other, forcing any invariant vector to assign the same weight to all states.
