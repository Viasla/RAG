# Interpretation
The coefficient of z^{j} in h(z) equals the probability p(j); thus h(z) serves as a compact repository of the entire distribution.

Derivatives of h at 0 recover probabilities via p(j)=h^{(j)}(0)/j! (Taylor’s formula).

The values h(1) and its derivatives at 1 provide normalization and moments, linking the generating function to expectation and variance.
