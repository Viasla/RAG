In the quadratic case with p_0=.2, p_1=.5, p_2=.3 the mean is m=1.1, leading to a favorable game because m>1; the expected profit is 50(1.1+1.1^{12})-100\approx112.

When the number of acquaintances N is large and the persuasion probability p is small, the offspring distribution is approximated by a Poisson law p_j=e^{-m}m^j/j! with mean m=Np. For m=1.1 the program Branch yields d_{12}=0.599 and the probability of receiving a bonus after 12 generations is 1-d_{12}=0.401.
