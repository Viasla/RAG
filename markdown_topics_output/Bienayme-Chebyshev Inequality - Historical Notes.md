# Historical Notes
The Bienaymé-Chebyshev Inequality first appeared in a work by Bienaymé before Chebyshev, and it was later commonly referred to as the Bienaymé-Chebyshev Inequality.

Chebyshev developed the inequality to prove a general form of the Law of Large Numbers, as noted in the historical remarks.

The inequality itself was mentioned earlier in Bienaymé’s work and was later discussed by Maistrov in the context of probability theory’s history.

Bernoulli’s 1713 work Ars Conjectandi contained the first proof of the Law of Large Numbers, but not the inequality itself.
