# Statement
The chord length for a chord with endpoints on the unit circle whose polar angle difference is \(\alpha\) is given by the Law of Cosines: \(L=\sqrt{2-2\cos\alpha}\). This arises by treating the chord as a side of an isosceles triangle with equal sides of length 1 and included angle \(\alpha\). The law of cosines states that for any triangle with sides \(a,b,c\) and angle \(\gamma\) opposite side \(c\), we have \(c^2=a^2+b^2-2ab\cos\gamma\); setting \(a=b=1\) and \(\gamma=\alpha\) yields \(L^2=2-2\cos\alpha\).
