# Interpretation
The mean recurrence time r_i can be interpreted as the average number of steps (or time units) between consecutive visits to state s_i in the long run of an ergodic Markov chain.

The mean recurrence time reflects how frequently a state is revisited in the long run.  A larger stationary probability w_i implies a smaller mean recurrence time m_{ii}=1/w_i, meaning that the state is expected to reappear more often.  Conversely, a state with a low stationary probability has a long mean recurrence time.
