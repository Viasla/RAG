# Statement
The statement of the weak law is given: for independent, identically distributed random variables $X_1,X_2,dots$ with finite mean $mu$ and variance $sigma^2$, $P(|S_n/n-mu|ge epsilon)	o0$ as $n	oinfty$, or equivalently $P(|S_n/n-mu|<epsilon)	o1$.  This is called the Weak Law of Large Numbers to distinguish it from the Strong Law of Large Numbers, which is referenced but not stated here.

Let X_1, X_2, ... be independent identically distributed random variables with common finite expectation μ; then P( lim_{n→∞} S_n/n = μ ) = 1.

In the case of a fair coin, μ=1/2 and the statement becomes P( S_n/n → 1/2 ) = 1.

Strong Law of Large Numbers is referenced on pages 70 and 314.
