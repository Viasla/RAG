# Notation
\(S_n\) denotes the accumulated fortune after \(n\) plays.

\(E(\cdot\mid\cdot)\) denotes conditional expectation.

The symbols \(p\) and \(q=1-p\) denote the probabilities of heads and tails respectively in a biased coin model.

The nominal value assigned to a counter at depth $k$ is $(q/p)^k$, where $p$ and $q=1-p$ are the win probabilities of the two players.

Player A's adjusted fortune is the sum of the nominal values of his counters, while player B's adjusted fortune is the sum of the nominal values of his counters; these sums constitute the martingale variables.
