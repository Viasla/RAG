# Historical Notes
Chebyshev developed his inequality to prove a general form of the Law of Large Numbers; the inequality itself appeared earlier in a work by Bienaymé and was for a long time referred to as the Bienaymé‑Chebyshev Inequality (Maistrov, 1974).

The Law of Large Numbers was first proved by James Bernoulli in the fourth part of his Ars Conjectandi (published posthumously in 1713); Bernoulli’s original proof was much more difficult than the later proof using Chebyshev’s inequality.
