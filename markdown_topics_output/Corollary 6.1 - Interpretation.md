# Interpretation
The corollary quantifies how the distribution of sums and averages behaves for independent identically distributed trials: the mean of the sum grows linearly with n while its variance grows proportionally to n, whereas the average retains the common mean μ and its variance shrinks to zero as n increases.  The standardized variable S_n^* has unit variance and zero mean, providing a scale‑free measure of deviations from the expected total.
