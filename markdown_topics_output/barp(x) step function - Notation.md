# Notation
$\bar{p}(x)$ denotes the step‑function extension of the discrete pmf $p(j)$.

$\bar{p}_n(x)$ and $\bar{p}_n^{*}(x)$ denote the step‑function extensions of the $n$‑fold sum pmf $p_n$ and its standardized version $p_n^{*}$, respectively.

The moment‑generating function of $\bar{p}(x)$ is written $\bar{g}(t)$; similarly $\bar{g}_n(t)$ and $\bar{g}_n^{*}(t)$ correspond to $\bar{p}_n$ and $\bar{p}_n^{*}$.
