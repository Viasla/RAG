# Interpretation
The coefficients \u2113{n}{r} count combinatorial selections, giving a direct interpretation of each term in the expansion as a choice of positions for b among n factors.

In probability, \u2113{n}{r}/2^{n} represents the likelihood of r heads in n fair‑coin tosses, linking the theorem to the binomial distribution.

The equality of figurate numbers, combinatorial numbers, and binomial numbers observed by Pascal shows that the same triangular array governs geometric patterns, counting problems, and algebraic expansions.
