# Intuition
The "last return" to the origin splits the whole trajectory into a "returning" part and a "non‑returning" part. Because the walk is memoryless and symmetric, the two parts behave like independent random walks of the appropriate lengths.

The factor \(u_{2k}\) measures how likely it is to be at the origin after 2k steps, while \(u_{2m-2k}\) measures the chance that, starting from the origin, the walk avoids the origin for the remaining 2m‑2k steps. Their product therefore captures exactly the event that the final zero occurs at 2k.
