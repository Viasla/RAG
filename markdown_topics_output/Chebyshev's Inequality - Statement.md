# Statement
For any random variable X with finite variance V(X) and any ε>0, the probability that X deviates from its expectation by at least ε satisfies P(|X‑E(X)|≥ε)≤V(X)/ε².

In the specific Bernoulli‑trial example, with A_n=S_n/n, E(A_n)=0.3 and V(A_n)=0.21/n, Chebyshev’s inequality gives P(|A_n‑0.3|≥ε)≤0.21/(nε²).

Exercise 4 in the provided text requests that for a binomial experiment of 1,000,000 fair coin tosses, the probability that the number of heads (S) lies within three specific ranges be estimated using (a) Chebyshev's inequality and (b) the Central Limit Theorem. The ranges specified are: 499,500–500,500, 499,000–501,000, and 498,500–501,500.
