# Notation
$u_{2m}$ denotes the probability that a simple symmetric random walk returns to the origin at time $2m$; $m$ is a positive integer.  $inom{2m}{m}$ is the binomial coefficient counting ways to choose $m$ up‑steps out of $2m$ steps.  The factor $2^{-2m}$ represents the probability of any particular sequence of $2m$ independent $pm1$ steps, each with probability $1/2$.
