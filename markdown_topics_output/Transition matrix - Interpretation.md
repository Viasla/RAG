# Interpretation
Each row of $\mathbf P$ lists the probabilities of moving from the corresponding current state to every possible next state; multiplying $\mathbf P$ by itself corresponds to chaining two one‑step transitions, which is why the $(i,j)$ entry of $\mathbf P^2$ is the dot product of row $i$ of $\mathbf P$ with column $j$ of $\mathbf P$.
