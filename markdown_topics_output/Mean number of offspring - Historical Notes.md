# Historical Notes
The discussion of the mean number of offspring originates from the classic branching process problem introduced by Galton, where the probability of extinction and the distribution of offspring were first studied.

Keyfitz (1977) compiled empirical data on the number of female children born to Japanese women aged 45‑49 in 1960, estimating an expected number of girls per family of 1.837, illustrating an applied historical estimate of the mean number of offspring.
