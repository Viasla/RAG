# Notation
\(X_i\): the outcome of the i‑th independent trial.

\(S_n = X_1 + X_2 + \dots + X_n\): the sum of the first \(n\) trial outcomes.

\(\frac{S_n}{n}\): the sample average (also called the empirical mean) of the first \(n\) observations.

\(\mu = E(X_i)\): the common expected value (population mean) of each trial.

\(\sigma^2 = V(X_i)\): the common variance of each trial.

S_n = X_1+X_2+\dots+X_n denotes the sum of the first n random variables.

A_n = \tfrac{S_n}{n} denotes the sample average (or proportion of heads in a coin‑toss experiment).

μ = E(X_k) is the common expected value (or the mean of the distribution).

σ^2 = Var(X_k) is the common variance; in the non‑identical case σ_k^2 denotes Var(X_k).

M_n = \sum_{k=1}^n m_k with m_k=E(X_k) is the sum of the individual means.

ε>0 denotes an arbitrary tolerance for deviation of the average from the mean.
