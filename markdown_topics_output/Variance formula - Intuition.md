# Intuition
Variance measures how far outcomes of a random variable typically deviate from its expected value; a small variance indicates outcomes concentrate near the mean, whereas a large variance indicates spread.

The formula $V(X)=E(X^2)-mu^2$ is often called the “shortcut” or “alternative” formula because it is easier to compute than the definition $E[(X-mu)^2]$, especially when $E(X^2)$ is easier to find than the whole distribution of $X$.

When a random variable is multiplied by a constant $c$, the variance scales by $c^2$, reflecting that squaring a linear scale factor increases spread by the square of the factor.
