# Examples
For the ten‑year snowfall ranking (Table 3.7) the permutation of the numbers 1–10 is read off, and the records are at positions i=1,2,4 because the values at those positions are larger than all values to their left.

Simulation results for random permutations give average numbers of records: n=10 average 2.968, n=20 average 3.656, n=30 average 3.960. These concrete numbers illustrate typical record counts for modest n.
