# Notation
w – the fixed row probability vector; P – transition matrix; I – identity matrix; wP – left multiplication of P by w; P‑I – matrix whose left nullspace is considered; left nullspace – set of row vectors x such that x(P‑I)=0.

P – transition matrix of the Markov chain

x – column vector with Px = x

M – maximum component of x

x_i – i‑th component of x

p_{ij} – entry of P giving transition probability from state i to j
