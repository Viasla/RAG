# Statement
The formula that we call Bayes’ formula gives the probability of a hypothesis (or a parameter value) given evidence; it is the probability of the hypothesis conditioned on the observed data.

In particular, Bayes derived the expression for the posterior probability that the unknown probability p lies in the interval [a,b) after observing m successes in n trials: 
P(a ≤ p < b | m successes in n trials) = (∫_a^b x^m(1‑x)^{n‑m} dx)/(∫_0^1 x^m(1‑x)^{n‑m} dx).
