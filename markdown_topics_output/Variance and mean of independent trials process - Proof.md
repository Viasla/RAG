# Proof
The proof uses linearity of expectation to obtain E(S_n)=nμ, and Theorem 6.8 (variance of the sum of independent variables) to obtain V(S_n)=nσ². The standard deviation follows as the square root of the variance. For the average, A_n=S_n/n, the expectation scales by 1/n giving E(A_n)=μ, and the variance scales by 1/n², yielding V(A_n)=nσ²/n²=σ²/n; the corresponding standard deviation is σ/√n.
