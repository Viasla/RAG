# Notation
$P$ – transition matrix in canonical form.
$Q$ – $t	imes t$ transient‑to‑transient block of $P$.
$R$ – $t	imes r$ transient‑to‑absorbing block of $P$.
$I$ – identity matrix.
$N$ – fundamental matrix, $N=(I-Q)^{-1}=I+Q+Q^2+cdots$.
$B$ – $t	imes r$ absorption probability matrix.
$b_{ij}$ – entry of $B$, the probability of absorption in state $s_j$ starting from $s_i$.
