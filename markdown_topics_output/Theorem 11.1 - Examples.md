# Examples
Example 11.1 defines a Markov chain for weather in the Land of Oz and shows how to compute the two-step probabilities by forming the transition matrix P and multiplying it by itself to obtain P^2.  Example 11.2 continues this example, computing successive powers P^1 through P^6 and demonstrating that the entries of P^n give the probabilities of each weather type after n days, illustrating the theorem.
