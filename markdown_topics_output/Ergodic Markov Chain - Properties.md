# Properties
Every regular Markov chain is ergodic, because regularity (some power of \(P\) having all positive entries) implies the ability to reach any state from any other.

The converse is false: an ergodic chain need not be regular, as demonstrated by the two‑state alternating chain (Example 11.16) and the Ehrenfest urn model (Example 11.17).

Ergodicity is equivalent to the chain being *irreducible*; i.e., the state space consists of a single communicating class.
