# Intuition
Every transient state in an absorbing chain has a directed path to at least one absorbing state. Consequently, the chain cannot remain trapped indefinitely among transient states. The probability that the chain has not yet been absorbed after (m) steps decays exponentially with (m), because in each block of (m) steps the chance of remaining transient is at most a fixed constant (p<1). Hence, as the number of steps grows without bound, the probability of still being transient tends to zero, guaranteeing eventual absorption with probability one.
