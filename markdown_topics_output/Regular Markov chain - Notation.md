# Notation
P – transition matrix of a Markov chain; p_{ij} – one‑step transition probability from state i to state j; p_{ij}^{(n)} – n‑step transition probability, i.e., the (i,j) entry of P^n; P^n – n‑th power of the transition matrix.

P denotes the transition matrix of the Markov chain.

P^{n} denotes the n‑th power of the transition matrix.

W = \lim_{n\to\infty} P^{n} is the limiting matrix for a regular chain.

w is the common row vector of W (the stationary distribution).

c is the column vector of all ones.

p_{ij}^{(n)} denotes the (i,j) entry of P^{n}, i.e., the probability of being in state s_{i} after n steps when starting from state s_{j}.
