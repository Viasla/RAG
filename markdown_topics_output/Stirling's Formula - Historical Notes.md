# Historical Notes
De Moivre obtained the approximation to factorials that is now known as Stirling’s formula before James Stirling, but he did not determine the exact value of the constant that appears in it, namely $sqrt{2pi}$.

De Moivre remarked that the precise value of this constant was not essential for the application he had in mind, yet he acknowledged that knowing it “has spread a singular Elegancy on the Solution.”
