# Proof
Huygens proved this by modelling the gamble as a two‑player game in which each player stakes (a+b)/2 and has an equal chance of winning the total stake. The value of the game to each player is then (a+b)/2. Adding a symmetric concession that the winner pays the loser a consolation prize of b does not alter the value by symmetry. In the modified game, if player A wins, he collects the total stake a+b and must pay b to B, leaving him with a; if he loses, he receives b from B. Thus A’s possible outcomes are a or b with equal chance, and the value remains (a+b)/2.
