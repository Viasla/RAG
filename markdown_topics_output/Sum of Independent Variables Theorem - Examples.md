# Examples
Example 7.3 illustrates the theorem for two independent uniform(0,1) variables. Their densities are f_X(x)=f_Y(x)=1 for 0≤x≤1 and 0 otherwise. The convolution integral yields

f_Z(z) = \begin{cases} z,& 0\le z\le 1,\\ 2 - z,& 1<z\le 2,\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}

This triangular density is the classic distribution of the sum of two dice rolled independently.

Example 7.4 applies the theorem to two independent exponential(λ) variables. Each has density f_X(x)=f_Y(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x} for x≥0. The convolution integral (omitted here for brevity) results in f_Z(z)=\lambda^2 z e^{-\lambda z} for z≥0, the Gamma(2,λ) density.
