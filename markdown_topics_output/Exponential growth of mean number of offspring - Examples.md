In the branching process of Example 10.8, the offspring probabilities are $p_0=0.2$, $p_1=0.5$, $p_2=0.3$, giving $m=p_1+2p_2=1.1>1$.  Consequently, the expected number of individuals in generation $n$ is $1.1^n$, confirming the exponential growth property for this specific case.
