# Statement
Let $m$ denote the expected number of offspring produced by a single parent, that is $m=h'(1)=p_1+2p_2+3p_3+cdots$ for a discrete offspring distribution.  If $m>1$ the branching process is supercritical and the mean number of individuals in the $n$‑th generation, $m_n$, satisfies the recurrence $m_{n+1}=m_ncdot m$.  Solving this recurrence yields $m_n=m^n$, so the mean grows exponentially at rate $m$ as $n	oinfty$.
