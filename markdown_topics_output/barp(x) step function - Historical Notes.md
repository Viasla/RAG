# Historical Notes
The step function \(\bar{p}(x)\) is introduced in the proof of the Central Limit Theorem for bounded discrete random variables to allow the use of characteristic functions and Fourier transforms; the text refers to its graph as the step function for the distribution \(p(j)\) (see Figure 3 of Section 9.1).

A complete rigorous proof involving the step function \(\bar{p}(x)\) and its variants can be found in the classic work of Gnedenko and Kolmogorov, "Limit Distributions for Sums of Independent Random Variables" (1968).
