# Examples
Example 5.6 examines gender (female/male) versus political party (Democrat/Republican). The observed table has 24 female Democrats, and the marginal totals are 32 Democrats and 18 Republicans out of a total of 50 individuals, with a sample size of 28 females. The probability of the observed table under independence is \[ \frac{\binom{32}{24}\binom{18}{4}}{\binom{50}{28}} \].

To obtain the p‑value, the hypergeometric probabilities are summed for a = 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, yielding 0.00395. Because this value is small, the null hypothesis of independence is rejected.
