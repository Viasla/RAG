# Historical Notes
The text makes a brief reference that the Weak Law of Large Numbers is presented in this chapter and that a Strong Law of Large Numbers is described in an exercise (Exercise 15) but does not provide further historical background on the Strong Law.

The Strong Law of Large Numbers (SLLN) was proven in advanced probability courses as an almost sure convergence result, extending the Weak Law of Large Numbers.

An example of failure of the SLLN was constructed by David Maslen, involving a sequence of independent random variables with increasing variance.
