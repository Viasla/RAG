# Statement
For two distinct patterns A and B (neither a subpattern of the other) in the Penney‑ante game, the odds that the first player’s pattern A occurs before the second player’s pattern B are given by John Conway’s formula: 

p_A / p_B = (BB – BA) / (AA – AB),

where p_A is the probability that A wins, p_B=1‑p_A, AA and BB are the expected waiting times (or “pay‑offs”) when the game is played for pattern A alone or pattern B alone, and AB, BA are the expected amounts earned by a gambler who starts while pattern A (respectively B) has just appeared and then continues until pattern B (respectively A) occurs.
