# Examples
Example 4.22: Let (ω1,ω2) be uniformly distributed over the unit square. Define X1=ω1^2, X2=ω2^2, X3=ω1+ω2. The joint CDF of X1 and X2 is F12(r1,r2)=√r1√r2=F1(r1)F2(r2), demonstrating that X1 and X2 are independent. For X1 and X3 we obtain F13(1/4,1)=3/8 while F1(1/4)F3(1)=1/4, so X1 and X3 are not independent (and similarly X2 and X3 are not independent).
