# Examples
While the text does not provide an explicit numerical example for Prop. III, the argument applies to any pair of payoffs a and b with rational probabilities p/(p+q) and q/(p+q). For instance, if a gambler could win 6 dollars with 3 out of 4 equally likely elementary events and 10 dollars with 1 out of 4 events (so p=3, q=1), the expected value would be (3/4)·6 + (1/4)·10 = 7.5 dollars.
