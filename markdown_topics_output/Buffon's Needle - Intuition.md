# Intuition
The needle problem asks: if a thin needle of length L is dropped at random onto a plane ruled with equally spaced parallel lines a distance W apart, what is the chance that the needle crosses one of the lines?

Intuitively, the event of crossing depends on two random quantities: the perpendicular distance d from the needle’s centre to the nearest line (uniform in [0, W/2]) and the acute angle θ between the needle and the lines (uniform in [0, π/2]).

A crossing occurs whenever d ≤ (L/2) sin θ; thus the probability is the proportion of the (d,θ) rectangle that satisfies this geometric inequality.
