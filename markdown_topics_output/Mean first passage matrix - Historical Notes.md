# Historical Notes
The mean first passage matrix is introduced in Section 11.5 of the text as part of the study of ergodic Markov chains.

The concept of the mean first passage matrix was developed alongside the fundamental matrix in the study of finite Markov chains. Kemeny and Snell introduced the use of the fundamental matrix $Z$ to avoid eigenvalue calculations, while Frechet used $Z$ for computing limiting variances in the central limit theorem for Markov chains. The explicit connection between $Z$ and the mean first passage times was formalized in later works, including the exercises cited in the textbook (e.g., Exercise 17 and 19) which present proofs and derivations.
