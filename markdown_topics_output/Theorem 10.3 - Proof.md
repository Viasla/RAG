# Proof
The proof proceeds by bounding each moment μₖ.  First, by definition, |μₖ|≤∫_{−M}^{M}|x|ᵏf_X(x)dx.  Because |x|≤M on the support, we obtain |μₖ|≤Mᵏ∫_{−M}^{M}f_X(x)dx=Mᵏ, since the integral of the density over its support equals 1.  Thus the coefficients of the power series satisfy |μₖ|/k!≤Mᵏ/k!.  The ratio test (or comparison with the exponential series) shows that the series ∑_{k}Mᵏ|t|ᵏ/k! converges for all t, implying that the original series converges for all t.  Because the series converges uniformly on every bounded interval, termwise differentiation is valid; each term μₖtᵏ/k! differentiates to μₖt^{k−1}/(k−1)!, yielding g^{(n)}(t)=∑_{k=n}^{∞} μₖ t^{k−n}/(k−n)!.  Evaluating at t=0 collapses all terms except the one with k=n, giving g^{(n)}(0)=μₙ.  Hence g(t) is infinitely differentiable and its derivatives at zero equal the moments of X.
