# Proof
The proof follows directly from Theorem 11.8 applied to the regular matrix $ar{P}=	frac12I+	frac12P$. Since $ar{P}$ has the same fixed vectors as $P$, the existence and uniqueness of the strictly positive stationary row vector for regular chains carries over to all ergodic chains. The properties about row and column eigenvectors then follow from the one‑dimensionality of the left nullspace of $P-I$ and the fact that the only right eigenvector with eigenvalue 1 is the all‑ones vector. ∎
