# Examples
For \(m=1\) (two steps): \(u_2=\binom{2}{1}/2^2=2/4=1/2\). Indeed, out of the four possible walks of length 2, the two that start with +1 (\(+1,+1\) and \(+1,-1\)) stay non‑negative.

For \(m=2\) (four steps): \(u_4=\binom{4}{2}/2^4=6/16=3/8\). Among the sixteen walks of length 4, exactly six stay non‑negative throughout.
