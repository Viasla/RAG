# Notation
**P** – transition matrix of the Markov chain (r×r), 
**u** – initial row probability vector, 
**w** – stationary distribution satisfying **w** **P** = **w**, 
X_n – state of the first chain at time n, 
Y_n – state of the second chain at time n, 
**P*** – joint transition matrix on ordered pairs (s_i,s_j), 
T – first time X_T = Y_T.
