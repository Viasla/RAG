# Intuition
Corollary 6.1 states that for a process of independent trials with each random variable having the same mean μ and variance σ^2, the sum S_n of the first n trials has mean nμ and variance nσ^2, while the average A_n has mean μ and variance σ^2/n. This scaling property shows how the average converges to the common mean and how variability diminishes with larger sample sizes.
