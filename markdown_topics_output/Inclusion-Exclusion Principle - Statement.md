# Statement
Theorem 3.8 states that for a probability distribution P on a sample space Ω and a finite set of events {A1,…,An}, the probability of their union is given by P(A1∪A2∪…∪An)=∑_{i=1}^nP(A_i)−∑_{1≤i<j≤n}P(A_i∩A_j)+∑_{1≤i<j<k≤n}P(A_i∩A_j∩A_k)−⋯. 

The Inclusion-Exclusion Principle is discussed on page 104.
