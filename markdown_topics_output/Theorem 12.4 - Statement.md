# Statement
If Peter and Paul play a game of Heads or Tails of length (2m), then the probability that Peter will be in the lead exactly (2k) times is equal to (alpha_{2k,2m}), the probability that a random walk of length (2m) has its last equalization at time (2k). That is, (b_{2k,2m} = alpha_{2k,2m}) for all integers (0 le k le m).
