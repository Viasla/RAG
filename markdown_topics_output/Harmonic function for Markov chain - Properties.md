# Properties
f is a fixed point of the transition operator: P f = f.

All powers of P preserve f: P^n f = f for every integer n≥0.

For absorbing chains, the components of f on transient states are uniquely determined by the absorption probabilities and the values of f on absorbing states.

Constant functions are trivially harmonic; more generally, any linear combination of harmonic functions is harmonic.

Harmonic functions are closely related to martingales in the context of finite Markov chains.
