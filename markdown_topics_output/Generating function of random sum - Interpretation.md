# Interpretation
The composition h(z)=g(f(z)) provides a compact way to derive the full distribution of a random sum without enumerating all possible combinations of N and the X_i’s. By differentiating h(z) at z=1 one obtains moments of S_N, such as its mean E[S_N]=g'(1)\,E[X_1] and variance Var(S_N)=g''(1)E[X_1]^2+g'(1)Var(X_1).

In branching processes, the same principle explains why the generating function after k generations is the k‑fold self‑composition f^{\circ k}(z), reflecting the iterative nature of offspring production.
