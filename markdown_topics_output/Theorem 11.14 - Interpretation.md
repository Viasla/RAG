# Interpretation
The theorem asserts that a regular Markov chain is ergodic: its long-run behavior is completely determined by a unique stationary distribution **w**, regardless of how the chain starts. The convergence of **u** **P**ⁿ to **w** guarantees that empirical frequencies of states converge to the components of **w**, and that any state will be visited infinitely often with asymptotic frequency **w**_j.
