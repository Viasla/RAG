# Intuition
In an ergodic chain every state is visited infinitely often, so the long‑run proportion of time spent in each state must be strictly positive. The theorem shows that there is exactly one such probability vector w that satisfies $wP=w$, and that no other row vector can remain unchanged under P unless it is a scalar multiple of w. The fact that any right eigenvector with eigenvalue 1 is constant reflects that the chain has no nontrivial recurrent substructures and all states are essentially equivalent in the limit.
