# Interpretation
The extinction probability d quantifies the chance that the chain‑letter scheme will terminate with no further participants before reaching an infinite number of generations.

The quantity 1-d_{12} represents the chance that at least one participant appears in the 12th generation, i.e., that the scheme survives twelve rounds.

The expected profit formula 50(m+m^{12})-100 captures the net gain from the initial investment of $100 given the expected number of letters sold in the first and twelfth generations.
