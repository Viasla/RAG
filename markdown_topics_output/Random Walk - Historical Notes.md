# Historical Notes
The concept of a random walk is introduced in Chapter 12 after several preceding chapters studied sums of random variables; the shift from merely describing distributions to analyzing the sequence of partial sums marks the historical development of random walk theory within the text.

The chapter situates random walks in Euclidean space (\mathbf{R}^m) and connects them to earlier probabilistic ideas such as independent experiments and multiplication of probabilities for finite outcome sequences.
