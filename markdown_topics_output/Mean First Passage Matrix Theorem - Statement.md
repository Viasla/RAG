# Statement
For an ergodic Markov chain with transition matrix $P$, stationary row vector $w$, and fundamental matrix $Z=(I-P+W)^{-1}$, the mean first‑passage matrix $M$ is given entrywise by
$$m_{ij}=\frac{z_{jj}-z_{ij}}{w_j},\qquad i,j=1,\dots,n,$$
where $z_{ij}$ denotes the $(i,j)$ entry of $Z$ and $w_j$ is the $j$‑th component of $w$.
