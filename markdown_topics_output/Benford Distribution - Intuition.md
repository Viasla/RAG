# Intuition
The Benford distribution arises because many naturally occurring data sets span several orders of magnitude, so a change of measurement units (a scaling of the data) should not alter the relative frequencies of leading digits; the only distribution that remains unchanged under such scale transformations is the Benford distribution.

Consequently, if numbers are fabricated without awareness of this law, they tend to be more uniformly distributed across digits, making the Benford pattern a useful indicator of authenticity.
