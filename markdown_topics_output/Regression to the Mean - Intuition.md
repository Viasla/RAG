# Intuition
Regression to the mean describes a situation in which an extreme observation in one generation tends to be followed by a less extreme observation in the next generation, drawing the values toward the average of the population. The underlying intuition is that the total contribution to a trait from many independent sources (genes, environmental factors, etc.) tends to balance out across generations, so that extreme parental values are counterbalanced by the averaging effect of the genetic and environmental mixture in offspring.
