# Interpretation
Theorem 11.3 guarantees that an absorbing Markov chain will, with probability 1, eventually be absorbed into one of its absorbing states. This result is fundamental for computing absorption probabilities, expected time to absorption, and other long‑term characteristics via the fundamental matrix and the matrix ( mathbf{B} = mathbf{N}mathbf{R} ). It ensures that analyses based on these matrices are well‑defined because they rely on the almost‑sure convergence of the transient dynamics to absorption.
