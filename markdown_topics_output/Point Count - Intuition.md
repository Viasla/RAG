# Intuition
The point count represents the total value of a bridge hand by summing the individual values of its 13 cards. Treating each card draw as an independent trial with a common distribution for card values, the overall point count is the sum of 13 independent integer‑valued random variables, analogous to the sum of dice rolls considered earlier. This framing allows calculation of the probability distribution of the point count and the likelihood of achieving a certain threshold, such as an opening bid.
