# Notation
\phi – the angle between the reflected ray and the line perpendicular to the wall through the mirror axis; X – the distance on the wall from the closest point to the mirror axis; B – a positive real number used as a placeholder for a value of X; the density is denoted f(B) or f(x).

$f_X(x)=\frac{1}{\pi(1+x^2)}$ denotes the Cauchy probability density function.

$k_X(\tau)=e^{-|\tau|}$ denotes the characteristic function of a Cauchy random variable.

$\mu_2$ denotes the second moment (variance when finite) of a distribution; for the Cauchy density $\mu_2=\infty$.

$A_n=\frac{X_1+\dots+X_n}{n}$ denotes the sample average of $n$ i.i.d. Cauchy variables.
