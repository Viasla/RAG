# Notation
λ – arrival rate (reciprocal of mean inter‑arrival time); μ – service rate (reciprocal of mean service time); ρ – traffic intensity, defined as λ/μ or equivalently the ratio of average service time to average inter‑arrival time; N – expected number of customers in the queue (queue length); T – expected waiting time of a customer in the queue.  Little's law is expressed as N = λ T, or equivalently T = N/λ.

N – expected number of customers (queue length) in the system; λ – arrival rate; μ – service rate; ρ – traffic intensity defined as λ/μ; T – expected waiting time of a customer.
