# Statement
For n \ge 1, the probabilities \{u_{2k}\} and \{f_{2k}\} satisfy the relation u_{2n}=f_{0}u_{2n}+f_{2}u_{2n-2}+\cdots+f_{2n}u_{0}.

Theorem 12.2 provides an example of a recursion that relates the (m)th term in an unknown sequence to earlier terms in the same sequence and to terms of one or more known sequences.
