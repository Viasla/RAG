# Notation
• 0 denotes the starting point of a one‑dimensional walk.

• (0,0) denotes the starting point in two dimensions.

• The return time is the number of steps taken by the walker until it first returns to its starting point.

• For a two‑dimensional walk, the meeting of two independent walkers after n steps can be represented as a single walk that starts at (0,0) and returns after 2n steps.

• Each step in a d‑dimensional walk moves the walker by ±1 in one of the d coordinate directions with equal probability 1/(2d).
