# Historical Notes
Pólya considered the problem of whether a random walker is guaranteed to return to its starting point for walks on the integer lattice in dimensions one, two, and three.  He established the result that in one and two dimensions the walker is certain to return, but in three dimensions the walker may drift away forever.

The result is often attributed to G. Pólya, who published his proof in 1921 and later discussed it in the 1970 collection *Scientists at Work: Festschrift in Honour of Herman Wold* (see the reference to “Two Incidents”).
