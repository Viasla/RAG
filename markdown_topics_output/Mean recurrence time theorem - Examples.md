# Examples
In the maze example (Example 11.25) the stationary vector is $\mathbf w = (\tfrac{1}{12},\tfrac{1}{8},\tfrac{1}{12},\tfrac{1}{8},\tfrac{1}{6},\tfrac{1}{8},\tfrac{1}{12},\tfrac{1}{8},\tfrac{1}{12})$. Hence the mean recurrence times are the reciprocals: $\mathbf r = (12,8,12,8,6,8,12,8,12)$.

For a weather model of the Land of Oz with stationary vector $\mathbf w=(2/5,1/5,2/5)$, the mean number of days between rainy days is $5/2$, between nice days is $5$, and between snowy days is $5/2$.
