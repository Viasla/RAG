# Notation
p denotes the probability of success on any single trial.

q=1−p denotes the probability of failure.

T denotes the number of trials until the first success.

Y denotes the integer satisfying 1−q^Y≥rnd, used for efficient simulation.

rnd is a uniform random number on [0,1).

⌈x⌉ denotes the least integer greater than or equal to x.

log denotes the natural logarithm.

T – number of trials until first success.

p – probability of success in a single trial.

q – 1-p, probability of failure.

p q^{k-1} – probability that the first success occurs on the kth trial.
