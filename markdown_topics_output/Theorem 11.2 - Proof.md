# Proof
The proof of this theorem is left as an exercise (Exercise 18).  In addition, the text notes that if we want to examine the behavior of the chain under the assumption that it starts in a particular state s_i, we simply choose u to be the probability vector whose i‑th entry equals 1 and all other entries equal 0.  In that case u^(n) gives the exact probabilities of being in each state after n steps when the chain starts in s_i.
