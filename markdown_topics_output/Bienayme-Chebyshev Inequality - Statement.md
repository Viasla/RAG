# Statement
For a random variable X with mean μ and variance σ^2, Chebyshev’s Inequality states P(|X−μ|≥k)≤σ^2/k^2.

In the context of Bernoulli trials with success probability p, let A_n=S_n/n; then P(|A_n−p|≥ε)≤p(1−p)/(nε^2).

In Example 8.3 with p=0.3 and ε=0.1, the bound becomes P(|A_n−0.3|≥0.1)≤0.21/(n⋅0.01)=21/n.
