# Examples
A program called 'General Simulation' was used to simulate 1,000 races of four horses, producing estimated winning percentages for each horse; the results matched the known probabilities within reasonable error.

A simulation of 10,000 plays of a coin‐tossing game produced a plot of the proportion of heads, illustrating the fluctuations predicted by the law of large numbers.

Monte Carlo simulations were employed by the Los Alamos scientists to model neutron transport in shielding materials, where analytical solutions were infeasible.

MonteCarlo (program) is referenced in the index.
