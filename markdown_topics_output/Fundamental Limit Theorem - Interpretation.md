# Interpretation
The theorem formalises the idea that for a regular Markov chain the state distribution after a long time is independent of the initial state: it converges to the stationary distribution $\mathbf{w}$. The matrix $\mathbf{W}$ represents the limit of repeated transition; each row of $\mathbf{W}$ gives the probability of being in any particular state after many steps. The positivity of $\mathbf{W}$ entries reflects the irreducibility and aperiodicity of the chain implied by regularity.
