# Properties
If the mean offspring m\le 1, the process is unfavorable: the extinction probability is 1 and the expected profit is non‑positive.

If m>1, the extinction probability d is strictly less than 1, and there is a positive probability of indefinite growth.

For a Poisson offspring distribution the generating function simplifies to e^{m(z-1)}.

The extinction probability after a fixed number of generations n can be computed recursively by d_{n+1}=f(d_n) with d_0=0.

Simulation results confirm that empirical frequencies of extinction and profit align with theoretical probabilities derived from the generating functions.

The variance of the total profit is larger in the Poisson case than in the finite‑distribution case, even when the mean profit is the same.
