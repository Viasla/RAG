# Notation
$X_n$ denotes the $n$‑th independent random variable in the sequence.

$S_n$ denotes the partial sum $X_1+dots+X_n$.

$mu_i$ and $sigma_i^2$ denote the mean and variance of $X_i$ respectively.

$S_n^*=rac{S_n-sum_{i=1}^nmu_i}{sqrt{sum_{i=1}^nsigma_i^2}}$ denotes the standardized sum used in the convergence statement.
