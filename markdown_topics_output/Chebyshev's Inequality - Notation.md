# Notation
X_j – indicator of success on the j‑th trial (1 for success, 0 for failure).

S_n = X_1+X_2+…+X_n – total number of successes in n trials.

A_n = S_n/n – sample average (empirical success probability).

ε – positive tolerance for deviation from the mean.

E(·) – expectation operator; V(·) – variance operator.

The text does not introduce a specific notation for Chebyshev's inequality itself; the only notation used is the binomial random variable (S) and its ranges.
