# Historical Notes
Bernoulli, in his study of Bernoulli trials, proved his famous law of large numbers. The theorem assured the experimenter that knowing the probability p for success, the proportion of successes would approach this value as the number of experiments increased. Bernoulli realized that in most interesting cases the value of p is unknown, and saw his theorem as an important step in showing that you could determine p by experimentation. The law has been foundational in the development of statistical inference and was later extended and applied by Laplace, Gauss and others.
