# Intuition
When the success probability p decreases, the geometric distribution becomes more spread out, producing larger values for the number of trials T with higher probability, as illustrated in Figure 5.1.

For any 0<p<1 the most probable value of T is always 1, because the ratio P(T=j+1)/P(T=j)=q<1 guarantees that each successive probability is strictly smaller.

The spread of the geometric distribution increases as the probability of success p decreases; consequently the variance q/p^2 grows rapidly when p is small.
