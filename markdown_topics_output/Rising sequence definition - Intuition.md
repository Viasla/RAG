# Intuition
A rising sequence captures a block of cards that appear in increasing numerical order and cannot be extended on either side while preserving that property. It represents a run of consecutive integers that rises as one progresses through the ordering, and maximality ensures that the sequence cannot be extended without breaking the increasing pattern. The example ordering (2,3,5,1,4,7,6) shows three rising sequences: (2,3,5), (1,4,7), and (6).
