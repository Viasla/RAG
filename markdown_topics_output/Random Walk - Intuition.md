# Intuition
Intuitively, a random walk is a succession of independent steps whose directions and lengths are governed by a fixed probability distribution; the overall path is the cumulative result of these unbiased or biased steps.

For the simple fair‑coin walk on the line, each flip contributes +1 (head) or –1 (tail), so after n flips the net displacement is the number of heads minus the number of tails.

Visualizing the walk as a polygonal line in the (time, position) plane helps to see the path: plot the points (k, S_k) for k=0,1,…,n and connect successive points with straight segments.
