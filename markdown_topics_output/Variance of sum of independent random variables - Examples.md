# Examples
Example 6.17 calculates the variance of a single die roll, yielding V(X)=35/12. Although this example does not involve a sum of independent variables, it demonstrates the use of the definition V(X)=E((X−μ)^2) and the alternative formula V(X)=E(X^2)−μ^2. 
In the independent trials process of Theorem 6.9, the variance of the sum of n independent rolls of a die would be n·35/12, illustrating the additive nature of variance for independent variables.
