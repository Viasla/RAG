# Interpretation
The theorem says that the chance of staying strictly on one side of the origin for $2m$ steps is the same as the central binomial coefficient divided by $2^{2m}$; this is the classic Catalan‑type result that Dyck paths (paths that never go below the axis) are counted by $inom{2m}{m}$.  Hence the probability of no equalization decays like $1/\sqrt{\pi m}$ as $m\to\infty$, reflecting the $\sqrt{m}$ growth of the expected number of equalizations.
