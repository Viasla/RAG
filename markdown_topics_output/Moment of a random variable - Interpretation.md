# Interpretation
The mgf acts as a generating series for the moments; its Taylor expansion about (t=0) has coefficients (mu_k/k!).  Differentiating (g(t)) and evaluating at zero extracts each moment, providing a convenient way to compute mean, variance, and higher moments without directly summing powers of the variable.  Because all moments appear in the mgf, knowledge of the mgf is equivalent to knowledge of the full distribution for discrete variables with finite support.
