# Historical Notes
The text notes that the definition of a distribution function for countably infinite sample spaces is obtained by extending the finite‑space Definition 1.2, requiring the total sum to be a convergent infinite series; this extension was not needed for finite spaces but becomes essential when Ω is countably infinite.

In the text the impossibility of defining a uniform distribution function on a countably infinite sample space is highlighted, referring to Definition 1.3 and illustrating a historical limitation of early probability theory.

The text notes that the word "distribution" is used in several ways in probability, both for discrete random variables and for continuous random variables, and that for a continuous random variable the cumulative distribution function is often called the distribution function.
